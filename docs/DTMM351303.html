System Description

  • The combined headlights supports signal prompt, which generates light signals to warn other drivers and pedestrians, including turn signals, hazard warning signals and clearance signals.

  • In addition to the traditional lighting functions, the vehicle is also equipped with automatic lighting and headlight delay off, which makes the lighting more convenient and humanized.

System Functions

  • Automatic light: turn the light switch group to AUTO. The left domain control module will determine according to the external light intensity collected by the light intensity sensor, automatically control the light (on and off), and turn on the clearance light or headlight according to the light intensity.

  • Headlight delay off: when the headlights are turned on and the vehicle power is switched from ON to OFF, the headlights will not go out immediately. The light switch group will automatically count the time, disconnect the light relay after the headlight stays on for 10s, and then turn off the headlights.
