System Function Principle

  1. Functions:

    1. Seat belt is a safety device to restrain the passengers in the event of impact, to avoid the secondary impact between the passengers and the steering wheel or instrument panel, or to prevent the passengers from throwing out of the vehicle.

    2. The vehicle, driver and passengers have kinetic energy during driving, the strength of which depends on the vehicle speed and the masses of the vehicle, driver and passengers. The greater the speed and mass, the stronger the energy released during impact.

    3. In case of vehicle impact, the driver and passengers without seat belts will still move forward at the speed before the impact due to inertia, however the vehicle does not move at this time, which may cause serious injury to the driver and passengers.

    4. Once the seat belt is fastened, in case of vehicle impact, the seat belt can restrain the driver and passengers in the appropriate position, reduce the inertia of the driver and passengers moving forward, prevent them from being thrown out, and make them get the best protection of airbags, so as to reduce the impact injury as much as possible.

    5. The front and middle-row seat belts are equipped with pretensioners, which are mixed into the retractors and controlled by the SRS electronic control unit. The pretensioner is a part of the Supplemental Restraint System component. When the frontal impact force exceeds the specified value, the pretensioner and airbag will be triggered at the same time.

    6. In case of serious frontal impact, if the triggering conditions of the pretensioner are met, the pretensioner will quickly retract part of the seat belt and strengthen the protection. The pretensioner limits the restraint force of the seat belt within a certain range, so as to avoid injury to the passenger.

  2. Composition:

    1. Main structures of seat belt: strap, retractor, buckle, latch, etc.

    2. The front and middle seat belts are equipped with pretensioners.
