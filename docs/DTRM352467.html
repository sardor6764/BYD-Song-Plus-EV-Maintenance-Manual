Test of Cooling System Pressure

Testing Cooling System Pressure

  1. Subsequent work should be carried out after the vehicle is completely cooled down.

  2. Remove the end cover of the expansion tank assembly I.

  3. Add coolant until the level fall between the “MAX” and “MIN” marks.

  4. Test the cooling system pressure with a special tool (tool name: cooling system pressure measuring tool; material number: xxxx). See the Manual of Special Tools.

  5. Apply a pressure of xx kPa.

  6. Check for pressure drop, and check the connections of cooling pipes for leakage.

  7. Remove the cooling system pressure measurement tool.

  8. Install the filler cap.
