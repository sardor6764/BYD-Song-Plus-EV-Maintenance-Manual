Steering Angle Calibration

  1. Preconditions for steering angle calibration: after the replacement of the steering wheel, the electric power steering column and universal joint, or the mechanical steering gear with tie rod, or after four-wheel alignment, recalibration of the steering angle of the EPS system is required.

  2. Matters need attention during calibration:

    1. The tire pressure and the loading state are normal, the vehicle is supported by its own wheels, and the driver alone must sit in the vehicle.

    2. Four-wheel alignment shall be completed before EPS system calibration.

    3. The vehicle shall be free of obvious vibration, for example, closing doors or front compartment cover is not allowed, and do not operate the steering wheel or apply any torque on the steering wheel.

    4. Check and confirm the steering wheel is in the mechanical zero position.

    5. After all above conditions are met, the calibration personnel shall tap the EPS calibration equipment command to start EPS steering angle sensor calibration.

    6. Steering angle sensor reading (EPS angle reading according to the equipment after steering angle calibration, with an allowable deviation of 0±3°).

    7. After the calibration, clear DTCs in the EPS system, repower on, and check the EPS system for DTC existence.
