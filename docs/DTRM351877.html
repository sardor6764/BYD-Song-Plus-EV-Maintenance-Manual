Right Rear Side Wall Glass Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the trunk lid assembly. See Trunk Lid Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the tool box assembly in the middle of the trunk. See Tool Box Assembly in the Middle of the Trunk - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the rear wall pedal assembly. See Rear Wall Step Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the left rear seat cushion assembly. See Left Rear Seat Cushion Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove trunk lid front end support plate assembly. See Trunk Lid Front End Support Plate Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the right rear door sill guard assembly. See Right Rear Door Sill Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the right rear side wall guard assembly. See Right Rear Side Wall Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the right C-pillar upper guard assembly. See Right C-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the right rear side wall roof batten assembly. See Right Rear Side Wall Roof Batten Assembly - Removal and Installation

  10. Take protective measures on vehicles.

    1. Paste protective tapes on the body around the right rear side wall glass assembly to protect the vehicle from damage during the removal process.

  11. Remove the right rear side wall glass assembly.

    Caution

    • This operation shall be completed by 2 persons.

    • Attention, there are 3 corner glass studs in the dotted box, avoid this area during cutting.

    • Ensure the protective tape is pasted in proper positions so as to provide the body with effective protection.

    • When cutting the rear glass assembly, never damage the vehicle coating.

    1. Cut a hole at the corner of the right rear side wall glass assembly with an awl, insert a fret saw into the hole and fix the two ends of the fret saw onto the handles.

    2. Pull the fret saw along the periphery of the right rear side wall glass assembly to cut off the glass seal.

    3. Take out the right rear side wall glass assembly.

    4. Clean and remove residual glass sealant on the body mounting face with a scrapper, so that the thickness falls within 1~2mm.

      Caution

      • Never damage the body surface.

      • Never damage the body paint. Otherwise, the bonding effect will be affected.

      Reminder

      Leave about 1-2 mm glass cement on the body for better installation of the rear glass assembly.

    5. Remove debris inside the vehicle and clean the body.

Installation

  1. Load the right rear side wall glass assembly to the installation position.

    Caution

    • Align the 3 glass stud on the rear glass assembly with the mounting holes on the body.

    • The rear glass assembly shall perfectly contact with the installation surface. Otherwise, further trimming is required.

  2. After inspection, take out the right rear side wall glass assembly.

  3. Clean the bonding surface of the body with glass cleaner (or alcohol) and let it dry for 10 min, after cleaning, contacting grease and water shall be avoided.

  4. Evenly apply 15mm-wide primer on the bonding surfaces between the glass and the body with a brush.

  5. Apply uniform-section glass cement evenly on the right rear side wall glass assembly.

    Reminder
    Apply uniform-section glass cement evenly along the glass cement application path on the rear side wall glass assembly.

  6. Align 3 glass stud mounting holes, install the right rear side wall glass assembly, and press gently along the edge.

    Caution

    • Adjust the glass position to ensure even clearance.

    • Gently tap the glass surface and the strip so that the glass can completely adhere to the body.

    • Sealant application and installation shall be completed within 10 min so as to ensure the adhesive effect of the sealant.

  7. Press the surface of the right rear side wall glass assembly gently, make sure the same is securely attached to the body.

  8. Secure the right rear side wall glass assembly with protective tapes until the glass cement is cured.

    Reminder

    • For the minimum waiting time before driving the vehicle, follow the instructions of glass sealant manufactures or the relevant manual.

    • Drying with compressed air is prohibited.

    • Drying with UV light and similar equipment is prohibited.

    • Sudden closing of doors will form a closed space, and result in instant high pressure inside the vehicle. Therefore, before the glass cement is fully dried, do not close doors suddenly, so as to avoid glass loosening and falling off.

  9. Remove the protective tape.

  10. After the sealant is cured, spray water (for 10min) from the outside of the vehicle to check for water leakage.

    Caution

    • In case of leakage, clean water stains and repair glass sealant from the mounting position inside the vehicle.

    • Using high-pressure water jet for spray test is prohibited.

  11. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
