System Description

  1. According to different configurations, the driver seat of this model is divided into 8-way electrical control and 6-way electrical control.

  2. The driver seat components include: ECU, guard, electric adjustment switch body, and adjustment button. In addition, the driver seat also includes the seat belt buckle, airbag module, etc.

System Function Principle

  1. Seat adjustment:

    1. When adjusting the driver seat, the driver seat adjustment button controls the adjustment motor, which drives the mechanical structure to change the seat attitude. According to different signals input from the seat adjustment switch, the seat will be adjusted to different attitudes to meet the intention of the driver. The driver seat ECU supports timeout protection. When the continuous operation of the seat adjustment switch reaches 30s, the driver seat ECU will stop the adjustment. If the adjustment needs to be continued, operate the seat adjustment button again.

  2. Seat heating:

    1. As for seat heating, the driver turns on the seat heating function by operating the multimedia system, which sends the seat heating signal to the driver seat ECU through the CAN line. The driver seat ECU supplies power to the seat heating pad, which converts the current into heat to the surface of the seat cover.

    2. The heating function is divided into two gears: high temperature and low temperature.

  3. Seat ventilation:

    1. As for seat ventilation, the driver turns on the seat ventilation function by operating the multimedia system, which sends the seat ventilation signal to the driver seat ECU through the CAN line. The driver seat ECU supplies power to the seat ventilation fan to make the seat ventilation fan work.

    2. The ventilation function is divided into two gears: high-speed ventilation and low-speed ventilation.
