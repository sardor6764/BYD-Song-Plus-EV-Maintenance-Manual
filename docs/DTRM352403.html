Left Rear Retro-reflector - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the left rear light guard assembly. See Left Rear Light Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the right rear light guard assembly. See Right Rear Light Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the left rear wheel eyebrow trim panel assembly. See Left Rear Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the right rear wheel eyebrow trim panel assembly. See Right Rear Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the rear bumper assembly. See Rear Bumper Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove one fixing screw.

  7. Pry up 2 clamping jaws, and take out the left rear retro-reflector.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
