Remarks

When checking item 1, if abnormal damage is found to the chassis parts, replace them in time.
Reminder
In order to maintain the power battery in its best condition, the vehicle shall be fully charged and discharged regularly (every 6 months or 72000km, whichever comes first) to complete battery self-calibration, or you may also contact BYD's authorized service shops for capacity testing and calibration.

  1. The service interval in the table is calculated from the date of purchase.

  2. To use the vehicle in the best way possible, please follow the mode below to operate the vehicle correctly.

    • Use ECO (economy) mode until the first maintenance.

  3. The following conditions are considered as harsh operating conditions:

    • The vehicle often drives in areas with a lot of dust or where the air is salty.

    • Frequent driving on bumpy roads, waterlogged roads, or mountain roads.

    • Driving in cold areas.

    • The electric motor often idles for long periods of time or often travels short distances in the cold season.

    • Frequent use of brakes, frequent slamming on the brakes.

    • Often used as a towing trailer.

    • Use as a taxi or commercial vehicle.

    • At a temperature above 32°C, the driving time in congested urban areas exceeds 50% of the total driving time.

    • At a temperature above 30°C, the driving time at a speed above 120km/h exceeds 50% of the total driving time.

    • Often overloaded.

  4. Required maintenance records.

    • You should let BYD authorized service stores complete all the necessary maintenance records in the forms and keep all the invoices for the maintenance services done on your vehicle.
