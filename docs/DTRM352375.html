Instrument Panel Upper Body Components - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the steering wheel assembly. See Steering Wheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the combination switch. See Combination Switch - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the glove box assembly. See Glove Box Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the instrument panel cluster assembly. See Instrument Panel Cluster Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the armrest box cover assembly. See Armrest Box Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the console body assembly components. See Console Body Assembly Components - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove 2 fixing screws.

  9. Detach 2 clamping jaws, and take out the inside temperature sensor.

  10. Disconnect 3 connectors.

  11. Detach one connector.

  12. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1N•m

  13. Remove 8 fixing bolts, and take out the instrument panel upper body components.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1N•m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
