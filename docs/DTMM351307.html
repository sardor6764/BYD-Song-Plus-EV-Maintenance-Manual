Air Conditioning System

System Description

  1. The system is mainly composed of electric compressor, condenser, HVAC assembly, refrigeration pipeline, duct, PM2.5 detector, evaporator and other parts. It supports refrigeration, heating, defrosting and demisting, ventilation, PM2.5 detection, and air purification.

  2. The mode damper, heating-cooling damper and internal and external circulation damper of air handling unit (AHU) are all motor controlled.

Principle of Refrigeration System

The gaseous refrigerant is extracted from the evaporator by the electric compressor and pressed into the condenser. The gaseous refrigerant is liquefied through the condenser for heat exchange (releasing heat), and the heat is taken away by the air outside the vehicle. The high pressure liquid refrigerant is depressurized by the throttling action of the expansion valve. The low pressure liquid refrigerant is vaporized in the evaporator for heat exchange (absorbing heat). The cooled air near the evaporator is blown into the compartment through the blower. The gaseous refrigerant is pumped out by the compressor and then pumped into the condenser, so that the refrigerant circulates in a closed way, discharging the heat in the compartment to the outside, and reducing the temperature in the compartment.
