HVAC Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Power off the high voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of High Voltage Electrical System (TZ200XSAC）

  3. Remove the console body assembly components. See Console Body Assembly Components - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly. See Electric Power Steering Column and Universal Joint Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front passenger seat assembly (electric seat). See Front Passenger Seat Assembly (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the console middle mounting bracket assembly. See Console Middle Mounting Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the coding cover. See Coding Cover - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the floor duct assembly. See Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the host assembly. See Host Assembly - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the ventilation cover assembly. See Ventilation Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  11. Refrigerant recovery. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

  12. Remove the pipe beam welding assembly components. See Pipe Beam Welding Assembly Components - Removal and Installation

  13. Remove the front passenger floor duct assembly. See Front Passenger's Side Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  14. Remove the driver's side floor duct assembly. See Driver's Side Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  15. Remove one fixing bolt, and disconnect 2 refrigeration pipelines.

    • Tightening torque: 10N • m

  16. Remove 2 elastic clamps, and disconnect 2 water pipes.

  17. Disconnect one connector.

  18. Disconnect one connector.

  19. Disconnect one connector.

  20. Detach 2 fixing clips.

  21. Disconnect one water pipe.

  22. Disconnect one connector.

  23. Remove 2 fixing nuts, and take out the HVAC assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 10±0.3N•m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
