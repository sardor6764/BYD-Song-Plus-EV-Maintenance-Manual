Inspection of transmission gear oil

Checking Oil

  1. Wait for 10 minutes and then raise the vehicle to make it level.

  2. Lift the vehicle. See Lifting Vehicle

  3. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Front Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Place a 1L measuring cup in advance, and remove the sight glass plug and sealing gasket.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2N•m

    Caution

    Stay away from components that are hot to prevent burns.

  5. Observe the sight glass and check whether the liquid level matches the correct position.

    1. A little oil overflowing from the sight glass is normal and nothing to worry about.

    2. There is no need to replenish the oil if it overflows from the sight glass in large amounts, which indicates overfilling.

    3. Check and fix any external leaks if the liquid level is too low and does not spill out of the sight glass. If there is no leakage, fill the oil through the oil screw hole until you see a small amount of oil overflows from the sight glass.

      Caution

      Recommended brand and model: Castrol BOT383 or verified equivalent lubricating oil. The transmission can be harmed by using oil that is not suitable.

  6. Install a brand new sight glass plug and sealing gasket.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2N•m

    Caution

    After the inspection, clean and remove residual oil on the transmission.

  7. Install the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Front Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Lower the vehicle.
