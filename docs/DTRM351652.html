Front Brake Pad Assembly (TZ200XYM) - Removal and Installation

Reminder
The removal and installation methods of the left and right front brake pad assemblies are the same. Take the left front brake pad assembly as an example.

Removal

  1. Remove the right front wheel assembly. See Wheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove one fixing bolt and detach the left front brake hose assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 22±2N•m

  3. Remove one fixing bolt and flip the left front brake caliper body assembly to the appropriate position.

    • Tightening torque: 30±3N•m

  4. Take out the left front brake pad assembly.

    Caution

    • Prevent inhalation of brake pad dust.

    • Never use suction hose or hairbrush to clean brake assembly, but use a vacuum cleaner.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

    Caution
    Conduct the road test after the installation, and the brake system shall work normally.
