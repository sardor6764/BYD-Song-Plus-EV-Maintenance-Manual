Checking Brake Light Switch Clearance

  1. Disconnect the brake light switch connector.

  2. Adjust the brake light switch clearance.

    1. Rotate anticlockwise and take out the brake light switch ①.

    2. Place a 1.5mm thick plate on top of the brake light switch limit pad ③.

    3. Insert the brake light switch into the mounting bracket ②, and keep the contact of the brake light switch fully pressed, turn clockwise and tighten the brake light switch, and pull out the plate.

  3. After the installation, press down and release the brake pedal several times. Measure and confirm the brake light switch clearance (i.e., the brake light switch contact exposure length) after the brake pedal returned to the home position ) is within 1.5 mm ~2.5mm. Otherwise, adjust the clearance.
