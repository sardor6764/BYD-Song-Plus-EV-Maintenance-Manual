Inspection of Ball Pin and Dust Cover of Steering Tie Rod

Reminder
左右两侧转向拉杆球销和防尘罩的检查方法一致，下面以左侧为例。

  1. Remove the left front wheel assembly. See Wheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Front Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Check whether the ball pin dust cover of the external steering rod is damaged and aged.

  4. Check whether the ball pin dust cover of the external steering rod is rotating flexibly without jamming or looseness, the nut is fastened, the locking pin is not lost, and the lubrication is good.

  5. Check whether the dust cover of the steering rod is damaged and aged.

  6. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install others.
