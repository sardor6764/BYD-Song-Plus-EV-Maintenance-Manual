Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • Wear a dust mask when maintaining the front brake to avoid inhaling dust from the brake pads.

  • During the maintenance of the brake system, avoid the oil contamination of the brake friction plate and brake disc. Otherwise, the braking effect may be affected.

  • Use an oil pipe wrench when removing and installing the oil pipes.

  • The brake fluid is highly corrosive. If it splashes on the skin, immediately wipe off the brake fluid with a rag, and then thoroughly clean it with soap and water. The brake fluid also has strong corrosion on the body paint. Avoid the brake fluid splashing on the body paint. If so, wash it with water immediately.

Warning

  • Never use compressed air or brush to clean the front brake assembly, but use a vacuum cleaner or brake cleaner.

  • Never reuse the discharged brake fluid. The brake fluid has strong water absorption and shall be placed in a sealed container.

  • When removing the front brake caliper, never press the brake pedal to avoid piston ejection and damage.

  • When replacing parts of the brake system, use parts with the same number or equivalent. Defective or substitute parts are not allowed.

  • When installing the front brake components, the fixing bolts or nuts of each component shall be tightened in accordance with the tightening torque indicated in this manual.
