Left Front Seat Belt Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the front compartment cover opening handle clips. See Front Compartment Cover Opening Handle Clip - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the front compartment cover opening handle. See Front Compartment Cover Opening Handle - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the instrument panel left end guard assembly. See Instrument Panel Left End Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the left front door sill guard assembly. See Left Front Door Sill Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the left rear door sill guard assembly. See Left Rear Door Sill Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the left B-pillar lower guard assembly. See Left B-Pillar Lower Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the guide ring. See Guide Ring - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the driver seat belt lower anchor point end cap. See Driver Seat Belt Lower Anchor Point End Cap - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the left B-pillar upper guard assembly. See Left B-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  11. Disconnect one connector.

  12. Remove one fixing nut.

    • Tightening torque: 44N • m

  13. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 44N • m

  14. Remove one fixing bolt, and take out the right front seat belt assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9N • m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
