Front Windshield Glass Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the left A-pillar upper guard assembly. See Left A-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the right A-pillar upper guard assembly. See Right A-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the digital video recorder camera. See Digital Video Recorder Camera - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the multifunction video controller assembly. See Multifunction Video Controller Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the inside rearview mirror assembly. See Inside Rearview Mirror Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the ventilation cover assembly. See Ventilation Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Take protective measures on vehicles.

    1. Paste protective tape on the upper edge of the front windshield glass assembly and the body on its both sides to prevent damage to the vehicle during removal.

    2. Cover the area around the air conditioner air vent with a piece of film, so as to prevent the glass splinters from falling into the air vent and causing hidden dangers.

  8. Open a hole at the corner of the front windshield glass assembly from the inside of the vehicle using a piercer, pass the fret saw through this hole, and fix both ends of the fret saw on the handle.

  9. Pull the fret saw along the perimeter of the front windshield glass assembly to cut the glass cement.

    Caution

    • This operation shall be completed by 2 persons.

    • Never damage the paint or interior/exterior trims when cutting the front windshield glass assembly.

    • Take care of that 2 glass studs are installed at the upper part of the front windshield glass assembly. Avoid them during cutting.

    • Ensure the protective tape is pasted in proper positions so as to provide the body with effective protection.

  10. Cut the surrounding glass cement in the direction of the arrow as shown in the figure.

  11. Take out the front windshield glass assembly with a glass sucker.

    Caution

    Check that the sucker is installed firmly, so as to avoid it falling down during installation and removal and causing component damage or even personal injury thereafter.

  12. Clean and remove residual glass sealant on the body mounting face with a scrapper, so that the thickness falls within 1~2mm.

    Caution

    • Never damage the body surface.

    • Never damage the body paint. Otherwise, the bonding effect will be affected.

    Reminder

    Leave about 1~2mm of glass cement on the body to allow for better installation of the front windshield glass assembly later.

  13. Remove debris inside the vehicle and clean the body.

Installation

  1. Place the front windshield glass assembly to the installation position with the glass sucker.

    Caution

    • Align the 2 glass studs of the front windshield glass assembly with the mounting holes on the body.

    • Keep the perimeter of the front windshield glass assembly in smooth contact with the mounting surface. Otherwise trimming is required.

  2. After inspection, take out the front windshield glass assembly.

  3. Clean the bonding surface of the body with glass cleaner (or alcohol) and let it dry for 10 min, after cleaning, contacting grease and water shall be avoided.

  4. Evenly apply 15mm-wide primer on the bonding surfaces between the glass and the body with a brush.

  5. Apply uniform-section glass cement evenly on the mounting position of the vehicle body.

  6. Align the 2 glass stud mounting holes, install the front windshield glass assembly, and then press it gently along its perimeter.

    Caution

    • Adjust the glass position to ensure even clearance.

    • Gently tap the glass surface and the strip so that the glass can completely adhere to the body.

    • Sealant application and installation shall be completed within 10 min so as to ensure the adhesive effect of the sealant.

  7. Gently press the surface of front windshield glass assembly to ensure that it is firmly installed on the body.

  8. Fix the front windshield glass assembly with protective tape until the glass cement is cured.

    Reminder

    • For the minimum waiting time before driving the vehicle, follow the instructions of glass sealant manufactures or the relevant manual.

    • Drying with compressed air is prohibited.

    • Drying with UV light and similar equipment is prohibited.

    • Sudden closing of doors will form a closed space, and result in instant high pressure inside the vehicle. Therefore, before the glass cement is fully dried, do not close doors suddenly, so as to avoid glass loosening and falling off.

  9. Take out the protective film and protective tape in the vehicle.

  10. After the sealant is cured, spray water (for 10min) from the outside of the vehicle to check for water leakage.

    Caution

    • In case of leakage, clean water stains and repair glass sealant from the mounting position inside the vehicle.

    • Using high-pressure water jet for spray test is prohibited.

  11. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
