Sunroof Blinder - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the ceiling assembly. See Ceiling Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the wiring harness assembly. See Wiring harness Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the air guide rod assembly. See Air Guide Rod Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the glass seal. See Glass Seal - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove 1 elastic clamps, and disconnect 1 water pipes.

  7. Remove 1 elastic clamps, and disconnect 1 water pipes.

  8. Remove 1 elastic clamps, and disconnect 1 water pipes.

  9. Remove 1 elastic clamps, and disconnect 1 water pipes.

  10. Remove the skeleton components.

    1. Remove 2 fixing nuts.

      • Tightening torque: 9±1N•m

    2. Remove 16 fixing bolts, and take out the skeleton components.

      • Tightening torque: 9±1N•m

  11. Remove 6 fixing screws, and take out the sunroof front horizontal skeleton.

  12. Detach 2 hooks.

    Reminder
    Move the blinder forward until the blinder guide block extends out of the guide rail.

  13. Detach 2 hooks.

  14. Detach one guide block.

  15. Detach one guide block.

  16. Lock the sunroof blinder with a special tool (tool name: XXX; material number: XXX). See the Manual of Special Tools

  17. Remove 6 fixing screws, and take out the blinder protective steel frame.

  18. Remove one fixing screw, and take out the blinder extension guide rail.

  19. Remove one fixing screw, and take out the blinder extension guide rail.

  20. Remove 2 fixing screws, and take out the blinder scroll gland.

  21. Take out the sunroof blinder.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
