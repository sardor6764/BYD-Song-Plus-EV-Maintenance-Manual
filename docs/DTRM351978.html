Front Compartment Thermal Insulation - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Connect a VDS and enter installation (factory) mode.

  2. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  3. Refrigerant recovery. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

  4. Remove the refrigeration tube assembly 1. See Refrigeration Tube Assembly 1 - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front subframe assembly. See Front Subframe Assembly (TZ200XSAC) - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the ventilation cover assembly. See Ventilation Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the front roof drip channel assembly. See Front Roof Drip Channel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Discharge the brake fluid. See Discharge and filling brake fluid

  9. Remove the instrument panel left end guard assembly. See Instrument Panel Left End Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the left front door sill guard assembly. See Left Front Door Sill Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  11. Remove the instrument panel right lower sound absorbing baffle assembly. See Instrument Panel Right Lower Sound Absorbing Baffle Assembly - Removal and Installation
