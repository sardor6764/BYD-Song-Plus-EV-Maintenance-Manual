System Description

  1. Integrated Power Brake (IPB) is electric brake system. It has faster response and more accurate control than the traditional vacuum booster pump brake system. It can significantly enhance the efficiency of braking energy recovery. IPB integrates ESP. Compared with the non-integrated brake system, IPB weighs less and occupies less space.

  2. IPB is mainly composed of integrated braking control system assembly, yaw rate sensor, wheel speed sensor assembly, instrument panel cluster, brake pedal assembly and braking pipeline.

  3. IPB includes the IPB body and associated parts, whose state affects the operation of IPB.
