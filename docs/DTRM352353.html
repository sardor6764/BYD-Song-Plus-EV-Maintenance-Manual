Instrument Panel Upper Body Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the instrument panel upper body components. See Instrument Panel Upper Body Components - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the center speaker mask assembly. See Center Speaker Mask Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the center speaker. See Center Speaker - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the 4GWiFiBT antenna. See 4GWiFiBT Antenna - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the two-in-one sensor. See Two-in-one Sensor - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the front passenger airbag module. See Front Passenger Airbag Module - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the channel temperature sensor (instrument panel left face duct assembly). See Channel Temperature Sensor (Instrument Panel Left Face Duct Assembly) - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the channel temperature sensor (instrument panel right face duct assembly). See Channel Temperature Sensor (Instrument Panel Right Face Duct Assembly) - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the instrument panel duct assembly. See Instrument Panel Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  11. Remove the two-in-one sensor. See Two-in-one Sensor - Removal and Installation

  12. Remove the instrument panel wiring harness II. See Instrument Panel Wiring Harness II - Removal and Installation

  13. Take out the instrument panel upper body assembly.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
