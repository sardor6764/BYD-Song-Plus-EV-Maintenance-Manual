Right Front Door Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the right front door inner snap handle cover. See Right Front Door Inner Snap Handle Cover - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the right front door module. See Right Front Door Module - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the instrument panel right end guard assembly. See Instrument Panel Right End Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the right front door sill guard assembly. See Right Front Door Sill Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the right front door wiring harness. See Right Front Door Wiring Harness - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    • Tightening torque: 28±2N•m

  8. Remove 2 fixing bolts, and take out the right front door assembly.

    Caution

    The right front door assembly is heavy. An assistant is required when removing the assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 28±2N•m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

  2. Adjust the right front door assembly. See Right Front Door Assembly - Removal and Installation
