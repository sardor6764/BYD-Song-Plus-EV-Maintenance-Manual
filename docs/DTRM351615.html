Wheel alignment

Caution

  • The kingpin caster angle and inclination angle of front wheel, and the front and rear cambers cannot be adjusted. In case of out of range, check whether the suspension components are bent or damaged. In case of bending or damaged suspension parts, replace the affected parts, and do not attempt to maintain the damaged parts by hammering or other means.

  • If the front wheel toe-in is correct, but the steering angle falls beyond the specified range or the internal steering angles are different, check suspension parts for bending or damage. In case of bending or damaged suspension parts, replace the affected parts, and do not attempt to maintain the damaged parts by hammering or other means.

  1. Inspection before four-wheel alignment:

    1. Release the parking brake to avoid incorrect measurement.

    2. Check and confirm no modification has been made to the suspension.

    3. Check and confirm the specifications of all four tires are the same and correct, and the tire pressure is within the specified pressure range.

    4. Check and confirm no person stay in the vehicle and the vehicle is free of excess load.

    5. Check each anchor point of the suspension for loose and deformation, and check and confirm the ball joint clearance of the suspension is appropriate.

    6. Check wheels for vibration and swing.

    7. Check and confirm the symmetric positions on both sides of the vehicle are in the same height.

    8. The steering wheel alignment tolerance is 0°±0.2°.

    9. Conduct the wheel dynamic balance test, and reinstall wheel back to the vehicle after the test.

    10. Let the wheel device jump for several times to stabilize the suspension.

    11. The test bench is in normal state.

    12. Adjust the toe-in of rear wheels first and then the front wheels.

  2. Detection

    1. Conduct wheel alignment inspection in accordance with the operating instructions of the four-wheel aligner.

  3. Method for adjusting rear wheel toe-in:

    1. Loosen 2 fixing nuts.

    2. Rotate rear control arm adjustment bushing until reaching the correct toe-in value.

    3. After adjustment, clamp the rear control arm adjustment sleeve to ensure that the toe-in setting does not change, and tighten the adjustment nut.

      • Tightening torque: 90±5N•m

      Reminder
      The left and right wheels are adjusted in the same way.

  4. Method for adjusting front wheel toe-in:

    Caution
    Center the steering wheel spoke.

    1. Loosen one elastic clamp.

    2. Clamp the flat part of the steering tie rod with a wrench.

    3. Loosen 1 fixing nuts.

    4. Rotate the end of the gear rack until the toe-in is correct.

    5. After adjustment, clamp the steering tie rod and tighten the lock nut. Make sure that the toe-in setting does not change.

      • Tightening torque: 70±5N•m

      Reminder
      The left and right wheels are adjusted in the same way.
