Right Front Drive Half Shaft Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the right front wheel assembly. See Wheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Front Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Disconnect one connector.

  4. Remove one fixing bolt, and move away the brake hose.

    • Tightening torque: 25N • m

  5. Disconnect the right front lower swing arm assembly from the right front lower swing arm ball joint assembly.

    1. Remove one fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 105N • m

    2. Remove 2 fixing nuts.

      • Tightening torque: 105N • m

  6. Detach the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly from the right front steering knuckle.

    1. Remove one split pin.

      Caution
      Cotter pins are disposable parts, which shall be scrapped after the removal. New pins should be used during installation.

    2. Remove one slotted nut and remove the right tie rod ball joint with a special tool (tool name: steering tie rod ball joint remover; material number: xxxx). See the Manual of Special Tools.

      • Tightening torque: 50N • m

  7. Remove one slotted nut, tap the end face of the drive half shaft with a rubber hammer, and detach the right front drive half shaft assembly from the right front steering knuckle.

    • Tightening torque: 235±15N•m

  8. Remove the right front drive half shaft assembly with a special tool (tool name: right front wheel half shaft remover; material number: xxx). See the Manual of Special Tools

    Caution

    • Avoid damaging the oil seal.

    • Never pull the half shaft forcibly during removal, so as to avoid crack of the CV joint dust cover.

    • Block the differential oil seal to prevent dust or foreign matters from entering the transmission.

    • Check the retaining ring and replace it if damaged.

Installation

Caution

  • Thoroughly clean the contact area between the half shaft and the transmission with solvent or brake cleaner, and then dry it with compressed air.

  • When installing the inner CV joint, support the outer CV joint and shaft rod of the half shaft by an appropriate tooling, so as to avoid half shaft fall-off, oil leakage or other problems caused by suspension in the air.

  • During installation, do not hold the sealing sheath by hand, so as to prevent wrinkling of the sealing sheath.

  • Install a new half shaft nut and tighten it.

  • Use an auxiliary tool to lock the half shaft nut into the wedge groove on the thread of the outer CV joint.

  • After the installation, check the front wheel alignment and make adjustment if necessary. SeeWheel alignment

  • Perform a road test.

  1. Protect the half shaft oil seal with a special tool (tool name: oil protective sleeve; material number: xxxx). See the Manual of Special Tools

  2. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
