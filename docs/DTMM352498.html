Terms

Term name Description of use Use examples
Removal Remove fasteners and components, such as bolts, nuts, screws, wheels, etc. Remove the wheel nuts.
Installation Install fasteners and components, such as bolts, nuts, screws, wheels, etc. Install the wheel nuts.
Disassembly Disassemble the assembly parts that can be disassembled. Disassemble the outside rearview mirror assembly.
Assembly Assemble and install the disassembled assemblies. Assemble the outside rearview mirror assembly.
Take out Take out the parts. Remove the wheel nuts and take out the wheel.
Adjust Align and reset the parts during installation. Adjust the steering wheel assembly.
Adjust the parameters of the system or components. Adjust the four-wheel alignment parameters.
Loosen Loosen but not completely remove it. Loosen the fixing bolts of control unit bracket.
Tighten Tighten the fastener. Tighten the fixing bolt of control unit bracket.
Pry up Pry up the locking pin and other parts. Pry up the bearing sleeve locking pin.
Disconnect Disconnect connectors, pipelines and other components. Disconnect the seat connector.
Connect Connect connectors, pipelines and other components. Connect the seat connector.
Open Open the door, sunroof and other components. Open the driver's door.
Close Close the door, sunroof and other components. Close the driver's door.
Detach Detach the wiring harness fixing clips, ties and other components. Detach the seat wiring harness fixing clip.
Lift down For heavy parts, several people shall work together to lift them down. Lift down the front subframe.
Lift Lift the vehicle, jack, etc. Lift the vehicle.
Lower Lower the vehicle, jack, etc. Lower the vehicle.
Shut off Shut off anti-theft, high voltage, etc. Shut off the anti-theft system.
Fill Fill the vehicle system with oil and fluid. Fill in coolant.
Discharge Discharge the vehicle system. Discharge coolant.
Exhaust Exhaust the vehicle system. Exhaust the brake system.
Recover Use equipment to recover media from the vehicle system. Recover the air conditioning system refrigerant.
Move away Remove components, but do not take out them. Move away the compressor assembly.
Press Press the seat belt, connector latch and other components. Press the seat belt latch.
