System Description

  1. The electronic parking system, through simple electronic parking switch operation, replaces the traditional manual pull rod. The rear domain control module controls the wheeled EPB motor drive assembly to realize the parking. And, the system can also assist safe driving.

  2. The electronic parking brake system mainly includes the electronic parking switch, rear domain control module, wheeled EPB motor drive assembly, wiring harness and others.

System Function Principle

Main functions of electronic parking system:

  1. Automatic parking: when the vehicle is powered off or shifted to Gear-P, the system will automatically enable parking.

  2. Manual parking: pull up the electronic parking switch, and the system will enable parking.

  3. Pressing the accelerator pedal to automatically release the parking: start the vehicle, and shift to Gear-D or R. the parking system will be enabled. Press the accelerator pedal gently to release the parking system according to the road conditions.

  4. Automatic release in gear shifting: start the vehicle and shift to Gear-P or Gear-N. The electronic parking will be enabled. Press the brake pedal and shift to Gear-R or Gear-D. The electronic parking will be released automatically.

  5. Manually release parking: start the vehicle, shift to non Gear-P, and press the electronic parking switch. The system will release the parking.

  6. Emergency braking: in case of braking failure during driving, the electronic parking system can be used for forced braking.

Caution

  • Never operate the electronic parking when the vehicle is running, unless the emergency brake is applied.

  • Never drive when the parking brake is not released.
