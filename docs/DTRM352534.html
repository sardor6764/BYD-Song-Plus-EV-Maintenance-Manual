Front Compartment Wiring Harness - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the front roof drip channel assembly. See Front Roof Drip Channel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the electronic accelerator pedal assembly. See Electronic Accelerator Pedal Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the left front guard of sub-instrument panel. See Console Left Front Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the right front guard of sub-instrument panel. See Console Right Front Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the front compartment cover opening handle clips. See Front Compartment Cover Opening Handle Clip - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the front compartment cover opening handle. See Front Compartment Cover Opening Handle - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the instrument panel left end guard assembly. See Instrument Panel Left End Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the left front door sill guard assembly. See Left Front Door Sill Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the instrument panel right lower sound absorbing baffle assembly. See Instrument Panel Right Lower Sound Absorbing Baffle Assembly - Removal and Installation

  11. Remove the front bumper with accessory assembly. See Front Bumper with Accessory Assembly - Removal and Installation

  12. Remove the washer reservoir and motor assembly. See Washer Reservoir and Motor Assembly - Removal and Installation

  13. Disconnect all connectors.

  14. Remove all fixing bolts at the ground points.

    • Tightening torque: 9N • m

  15. Pry up all fixing clips and through-hole rubber sleeves, and take out the front compartment wiring harness.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
