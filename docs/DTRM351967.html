Rear Wall Step Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the trunk lid assembly. See Trunk Lid Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the tool box assembly in the middle of the trunk. See Tool Box Assembly in the Middle of the Trunk - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the tailgate frame weather strip. See Tailgate Frame Weather Strip - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove 4 snap fasteners.

  5. Pry up 4 fixing clips, and take out the rear wall step assembly.

Installation

Caution
Replace the damaged snap fasteners and fixing clips.

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
