Refrigeration Tube Assembly 1 - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Refrigerant recovery. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

  2. Remove one fixing bolt.

  3. Remove one fixing bolt, and detach the refrigeration tube assembly 1 from the condenser assembly.

    Caution

    • Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

    • The O-ring at the interface of the refrigeration tube is disposable, and needs to be scrapped and replaced with a new one after each removal.

  4. Remove one fixing bolt, disconnect the refrigeration tube assembly 1 from the integrated thermal management module, and take out the refrigeration tube assembly 1.

    Caution

    • Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

    • The O-ring at the interface of the refrigeration tube is disposable, and needs to be scrapped and replaced with a new one after each removal.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

  2. Refrigerant filling. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant
