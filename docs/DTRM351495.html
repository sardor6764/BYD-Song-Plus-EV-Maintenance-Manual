Power Battery Pack - Visual Inspection Standard

  1. The appearance level and inspection of the power battery pack.

    1. Inspection of seals: sealing cover, tray, distribution box components (housing, high and low connectors and water nozzle), vent valve components (pressing plate, base, vent valve), rear drive mounting components (base, rear drive connector).

    2. Inspection of connection with the vehicle: lifting lug, L-shaped aluminum plate, mounting nut of the vehicle bottom guard, front and rear high and low voltage connectors, and water nozzle.

    3. Inspection of protective parts: foam, tray foam layer, guard.

    4. Inspection of fasteners: sealing cover fixing screws, trim strips, front and rear pressing plates, water nozzle fixing bolts, front and rear high and low voltage connectors, distribution box and sealing cover fixing bolts.

    5. Inspection of labels: positive and negative pole labels, nameplates, and warnings against stepping.

    6. Level S: the battery cell area at the bottom of the power battery pack, and the key area of the power battery pack, which have the highest risk of cell damage.

    7. Level A: the area covered by the sealing cover and the important area of the power battery pack, which have a high risk of cell damage.

    8. Level B: the area at the bottom of the power battery pack except the cell area, which has a high risk of damage and is generally important.

    9. Level C: the side of the power battery pack, which has an average risk of damage and is generally important.
