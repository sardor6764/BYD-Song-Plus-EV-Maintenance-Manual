Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

The small and medium probes may not work properly under the following conditions:

  • Soil or snow is attached to the small probe (After washing and removing foreign matters, the function is restored).

  • The small probe is blocked by other objects.

  • The external temperature is low, and the small probe fails due to icing.

The detection range of small and medium probes may change under the following conditions:

  • Soil or snow is attached to the small probe (After washing and removing foreign matters, the function is restored).

  • The vehicle is exposed to the sun or ultra-low temperature.

Small and medium probes may have false detection under the following conditions:

  • The vehicle is on a rough/gravel road or grass.

  • There are ultrasonic noises with the same frequency around, such as metal sound, high pressure gas emission sound, and vehicle horn sounding towards the small probe.

  • The vehicle is in heavy rain or splashed with water stains.

  • The vehicle inclines greatly.

  • Soil or snow is attached to the small probe.

  • The vehicle is equipped with a tow hook.

  • Another vehicle with a small probe approaches.

  • The vehicle is moving towards a higher or right angle curb.

Due to the shape, characteristics or materials of the obstacles, the small probe cannot detect the following items:

  • Linear objects, such as wire or rope.

  • Objects that easily absorb sound waves, such as cotton, snow, etc.

  • Objects with sharp edges.

  • Objects too low.

  • Objects too high or its upper part protruding.

  • The small probe is subjected to strong impact or collision.

  • When the vehicle is too close to the step, the system cannot correctly measure the distance between the bottom step and the rear of the vehicle.

Warning

Never process the small probe.

  • No painting make-up. No overall painting.

Small probe cleaning.

  • When washing the vehicle, never wash the small probe with high pressure water or steam. The small probe may be damaged.

Small probe maintenance.

  • The small probe is a precision vulnerable part. Avoid impact and scratch during maintenance.
