System Function Principle

  • This vehicle gets a panoramic sunroof. The sunroof control motor assembly, through the forward and reverse rotation, opens, closes and tilts up the sunroof.

  • The vehicle is also equipped with an electric blinder system, which can open and close the blinder according to the sunroof switch state and the blinder control switch.

  • The electric blinder and power window have anti pinch function.

  • When the sunroof/blinder is blocked by a large force during the closing process, the anti-pinch function will be triggered, and the sunroof/blinder will stop after retreating a certain distance.

  • When opening and closing the sunroof, never put your head or hands over there. Serious injury may be caused.
