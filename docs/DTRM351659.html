Right Front Brake Caliper Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the right front brake pad holder aftersales package. See Front Brake Pad Holder Aftersales Package

  2. Remove 2 fixing bolts, and take out the right front brake caliper bracket assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 30±3N•m

    Caution
    Hang the right front brake caliper body assembly safely to a position without causing interference, and prevent the front brake hose assembly from being excessively stressed.

  3. Remove 2 fixing bolts, and take out the right front brake caliper bracket assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 160±10N•m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

    Caution

    Reset the front brake caliper piston with a special tool (tool name: XXX; material number: YYY). See "Special Tools" for specific removal methods.
