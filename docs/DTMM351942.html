System Description

  1. According to different configurations, the front passenger seat of this model is divided into 4-way electrical control and 4-way manual control.

  2. The front passenger seat assembly includes: front passenger seat guard, front passenger electric seat adjustment switch body, and front passenger adjustment button. In addition, the front passenger seat also includes the belt buckle, airbag module, etc.

System Function Principle

  1. Seat adjustment:

    1. When adjusting the front passenger seat, the front passenger seat adjustment button controls the adjustment motor, which drives the mechanical structure to change the seat attitude. According to different signals input from the seat adjustment switch, the seat will be adjusted to different attitudes to meet the intention of the front passenger.

  2. Seat heating:

    1. As for seat heating, the front passenger turns on the seat heating function by operating the multimedia system, which sends the seat heating signal to the driver seat ECU through the CAN line. The driver seat ECU supplies power to the front passenger seat heating pad, which converts the current into heat to the surface of the seat cover.

    2. The heating function is divided into two gears: high temperature and low temperature.

  3. Seat ventilation:

    1. As for seat ventilation, the front passenger turns on the seat ventilation by operating the multimedia system, which sends the seat ventilation signal to the driver seat ECU through the CAN line. The driver seat ECU supplies power to the front passenger seat ventilation fan to make it work.

    2. The ventilation function is divided into two gears: high-speed ventilation and low-speed ventilation.
