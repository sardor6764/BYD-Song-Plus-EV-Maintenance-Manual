Inspection of Steering Wheel

  1. Check the steering wheel.

    1. Hold the steering wheel and shake it up and down, left and right, and check the steering wheel for swing, looseness, etc.

    2. The power of vehicle is in "OK" position. Check whether the steering wheel rotates heavily. If it is heavy, check the steering system.

  2. Check the free clearance of the steering wheel.

    1. Set the front wheels to the straight driving position.

    2. Power off the vehicle.

    3. Mark on the top of the steering wheel.

    4. Place a ruler against the top of the steering wheel.

    5. Measure the maximum left turning distance of the steering wheel with the front wheel stay still.

    6. Turn the steering wheel back to the original position.

    7. Measure the maximum right turning distance of the steering wheel with the front wheel stay still.

    8. If the sum of the two measured values does not exceed 30mm, indicate that the free stroke of the steering wheel is normal. Otherwise, check the steering system.
