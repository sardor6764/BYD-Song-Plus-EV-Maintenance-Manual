Exhaust of Brake System

This model is provided with two exhaust modes: wet exhaust and dry exhaust

  1. This vehicle adopts the Bosch intelligent power brake system. Follow the operating instructions to bleed the brake fluid lines if air gets in during the brake system maintenance. Otherwise, the trapped air may reduce the performance of the intelligent power brake system, trigger the brake system alarm, and compromise the braking safety.

    Caution

    • Never reuse the discharged brake fluid.

    • Pure HZY6 brake fluid must be used. Using unauthorized brake fluids may result in corrosion and shorten the service life of the brake system.

    • Never spill brake fluid on the vehicle. It may damage the paint. If brake fluid splashes on the paint, clean it with water immediately.

    • The brake fluid pressurization equipment and the VDS shall be used for exhaust. Before exhaust, check and confirm the brake fluid in the pressurization equipment is sufficient, and the filling pressure is set to 2bar (0.2mpa). The filler cap must be tightened. Otherwise, the brake fluid will eject out.

    • After exhaust, refill or drain excess brake fluid as required, and make sure the brake fluid level reaches the “MAX” marker on the brake fluid reservoir.

Wet exhaust

  1. Application scope of wet exhaust: when brake system exhaust is required after replacing or removing and installing brake calipers, brake hoses and break tubes.

    Caution
    When replacing the intelligent brake system, dry exhaust is required.

  2. Power on the Vehicle. Connect a VDS. Make the intelligent power brake system enter "installation mode". SeeEnter Installation Mode

    Caution

    If the above operation is not performed before replacing or removing and installing brake calipers, brake hoses and brake tubes, dry exhaust must be performed.

  3. Power off the vehicle.

  4. Replace or remove and install brake calipers, brake hoses and break tubes.

  5. Connect a clean and transparent exhaust hose to the vent screw.

  6. Connect the brake fluid pressurization equipment, and check and confirm the equipment and the brake fluid reservoir opening are sealed properly and fixed securely without leakage.

  7. Start the brake fluid pressurizer, increase the pressure to 2 bar, loosen the brake vent screw, depress the brake pedal normally once every 2 s until no bubbles can be seen in the brake fluid discharged from the drain hose (which indicates that the air is discharged completely), depress the brake pedal, and tighten the vent screw.

    Caution

    • Exhaust is required for hydraulic circuits that once been removed.

    • The filling pressure of brake fluid filling machine is 2bar. Never increase the pressure. It may result in brake fluid reservoir damage!

  8. Connect a VDS. Power on the vehicle. Make the intelligent power brake system "exit installation mode". SeeExit Installation Mode

  9. Execute "DTC clearing” for the intelligent power brake system . SeeClearing DTCs of Intelligent Power Brake System

  10. Check and confirm no ABS, ESP and brake system alarm is generated on the instrument panel, and no abnormal sound is heard when stepping down the brake pedal, with normal pressing feeling, it indicates successful exhaust. Otherwise, repeat the dry exhaust procedure.

  11. Shut down the brake fluid pressurization equipment, wait until the pressure decrease to 0, disconnect the pressurization equipment.

  12. Drain excess brake fluid from the brake fluid reservoir, make sure the brake fluid level reaches the “MAX” marker on the brake fluid reservoir.

Dry exhaust

  1. Application scope of dry exhaust: changing brake fluid; replacing the intelligent power brake system assembly; replacing brake calipers, brake hoses and break tubes without setting the intelligent power brake system assembly to the “installation mode”; wet exhaust failed.

  2. Power on the Vehicle. Connect a VDS. Make the intelligent power brake system enter "installation mode". SeeEnter Installation Mode

  3. Power off the vehicle.

  4. Perform the following operations as needed: change the brake fluid, replace, remove, or install the intelligent power brake system assembly.

  5. Connect a clean and transparent exhaust hose to the vent screw.

  6. Connect the brake fluid pressurization equipment, and check and confirm the equipment and the brake fluid reservoir opening are sealed properly and fixed securely without leakage. Start the brake fluid pressurization equipment and increase the pressure to 2bar.

    Caution

    The filling pressure of brake fluid filling machine is 2bar. Never increase the pressure. It may result in brake fluid reservoir damage!

  7. Power on the vehicle. Connect a VDS. And execute "left rear wheel exhaust command". Loosen the brake vent screw, and press down the brake pedal normally every 2s until the calibration is succeeded. When bubble coming out from the exhaust hose, indicate that air has been fully discharged. After the exhaust, press the brake pedal, and tighten the vent screw.

    Caution

    • In this process, the pedal may be difficult to be pressed down, in this case, the driver only has to hold the pedal, and press down the pedal violently is prohibited.

    • If bubble still coming out from the exhaust hose, repeat the above steps until no bubble comes out.

    • Never exit the VDS exhaust operating interface during the exhaust process. Otherwise, restart the exhaust process.

    • When a single caliper has two exhaust vents (vent screws), the two vents need to be exhausted separately, and the caliper should be operated twice (for fixed calipers).

  8. Repeat above operation for the left rear, the left front, the right front and the right rear wheels, until no bubble coming out from the exhaust house.

    Caution

    • Exhaust shall be carried out in accordance with the stipulated sequence. Otherwise, restart the exhaust process.

    • When the exhaust calibration process is terminated by ECU, restart the exhaust process. If exhaust fails after several attempts, stay on the failure interface and wait for 2 minutes before starting a new exhaust attempt.

  9. After the wheel exhaust, shut down the pressurization equipment, wait until the pressure decrease to 0, and disconnect the pressurization equipment.

  10. Conduct the "IPB housing exhaust” until calibration completes, pressing the brake pedal is not required in this process.

    Caution

    In this process, the pot cover shall not be installed on the brake fluid reservoir. It may result in brake fluid reservoir damage.

  11. Conduct “PFS and TMC2 exhaust”. Press the brake pedal normally every 3s until the calibration completes.

  12. Make the intelligent power brake system "exit installation mode". SeeExit Installation Mode

  13. Check and confirm no ABS, ESP and brake system alarm is generated on the instrument panel, and no abnormal sound is heard when stepping down the brake pedal, with normal pressing feeling, it indicates successful exhaust. Otherwise, repeat the dry exhaust procedure.

  14. Drain excess brake fluid from the brake fluid reservoir, make sure the brake fluid level reaches the “MAX” marker on the brake fluid reservoir.
