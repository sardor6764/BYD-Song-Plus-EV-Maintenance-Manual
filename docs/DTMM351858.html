Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

Important notes on vehicle lifting:

  • Ensure that the lifting machine is in good condition before lifting the vehicle.

  • Check whether the lifting position is correct. Never support any part except the lifting points.

  • When lifting the vehicle, there must be no people around and below the vehicle.

Important notes on maintenance:

  • During the operation or disassembly of the vehicle, the dust cover of the drive half shaft shall not rub with other parts for a long time or collide with sharp objects to avoid damage.

  • Avoid polluting parts during maintenance to prevent foreign matters from entering.

  • Clean the external surface of the parts before disassembly and assembly.

  • Clean up the disassembled parts (except rubber parts) shall with detergent and dry them.

Important warnings on road test:

  • Conduct the road test on the premise of safety and follow all traffic regulations.

  • Never attempt any operation that may endanger vehicle control.
