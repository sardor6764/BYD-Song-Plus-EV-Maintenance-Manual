Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • Except for the operations described in this manual, never use electrical test equipment to test any circuit of the supplemental restraint system.

  • When installing the airbag control module, ensure the correct installation direction to guarantee its correct operation. Before the installation, check whether there is crack, deformation or corrosion.

  • After a slight impact, check the supplemental restraint system even if the airbag is not deployed.

  • If the front passenger airbag module, side curtain airbag module, driver airbag module, SRS electronic control unit and other components fall to the ground, are vibrated, knocked, or have cracks, indentations or other damages on the shell, bracket or connector, replace them with new ones.

  • Warning labels are pasted on the components of the supplemental restraint system and relevant positions in the vehicle. During operation, follow the above instructions.

  • If the vehicle is likely to be exposed to vibrations during maintenance of other systems or components of the vehicle, the SRS electronic control unit should be removed prior to maintenance.

  • Remove the front passenger airbag module, side curtain airbag module, driver airbag module, SRS electronic control unit and other components and store them safely if they are affected by overheating (above 93°C) of painting operation

  • During the maintenance of the supplemental restraint system, the airbag module must be installed immediately after it is removed from the transport container. If the work needs to be stopped, the airbag module shall be put back into the transport container. The airbag module shall not be placed in an unattended place. When storing the removed airbag module, make the buffer surface upward.

  • Before carrying out specific maintenance, set the power to OFF. Remove the negative ground line of starter iron battery. Wait for more than 90s and then start the maintenance operation. Otherwise, the airbag may be accidentally deployed. In addition, wrap the removed negative pole of the starter iron battery with insulating tape.

  • Before removing the negative ground line of starter iron battery, make a record of the state of other systems, because trouble codes of other systems will be lost during the removal.

  • No matter where the electric welding is used on the vehicle, the supplemental restraint system must be disconnected first to avoid accidental deployment.

  • Never use the detection light, ordinary voltmeter and ohmmeter during detection. The voltmeter and ohmmeter shall be of high impedance (minimum 10K Ω/v). Never use the ohmmeter on the airbag module.

  • Before and after replacing any component, conduct the system self-diagnosis to comprehensively check whether the system function is normal.

  • After the front passenger airbag module is deployed, if the instrument panel is damaged, replace it.

  • SRS electronic control unit connector, clock spring connector, front passenger airbag module connector, side curtain airbag module connector and driver airbag module connector are all equipped with protection mechanisms to prevent accidental deployment of airbag.

  • After the supplemental restraint system is maintained, never rush to connect the airbag module to the circuit. Conduct electrical inspection first.

  • The service life of the airbag is 10 years. If the service life of the airbag is exceeded, replace the airbag and label.

  • The airbag deployment device contains sodium azide and potassium nitrate, which are toxic and easy to burn. If acid, water or heavy metals are encountered, harmful irritant gases (sodium hydroxide is easy to generate in wet places) or combustible compounds will be generated. The airbag device contains pressurized argon. Never disassemble the airbag device or the deployment device that damages the airbag. Never puncture or burn the airbag device. Never let the airbag device contact with electricity. Never store it in an environment over 93°C.

Warning

  • Never use supplemental restraint system components removed from other vehicles. Replace the parts when necessary.

  • Never disassemble and repair the front passenger airbag module, side curtain airbag module, driver airbag module, SRS electronic control unit and other components for reuse or other reasons.

  • Never put the front passenger airbag module, side curtain airbag module, driver airbag module, SRS electronic control unit and other components into hot air or flame.

  • Never disassemble and repair DAB, PAB, SAB, CAB or install airbag ECU for reuse or other reasons.

  • Never put DAB, PAB, SAB, CAB, airbag ECU and other components into hot air or flame.
