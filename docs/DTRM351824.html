Electric Power Steering Column and Universal Joint Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Make the front wheels and steering wheel in the straight driving direction.

  2. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  3. Remove the instrument panel left lower guard assembly. See Instrument Panel Left Lower Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the combination switch. See Combination Switch - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove universal joint dust cover assembly I. See Universal Joint Dust Cover Assembly I - Removal and Installation

  6. Detach 4 fixing clips.

  7. Remove the wiring harness.

    1. Disconnect 2 connectors.

    2. Detach 2 fixing clips.

  8. Remove one fixing bolt, and detach the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly from the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 60N • m

  9. Remove 2 fixing nuts.

    Caution
    Firmly support or lift the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly to avoid falling.

    • Tightening torque: 25N • m

  10. Remove the fixing bolts, and take out the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly.

    Caution
    Firmly support or lift the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly to avoid falling.

    • Tightening torque: 50N • m

Installation

Caution

  • When installing the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly, first install the long fixing bolt at the lower end, and then install the fixing nut at the upper end.

  • Align the missing-tooth mark when connecting the intermediate shaft assembly.

  • Confirm that the mounting bolt at the lower end of the intermediate shaft assembly can be accurately installed into the groove of the steering gear spline.

  • Ensure that the lower end of the intermediate shaft assembly is installed in place.

  • Install the bolt from the side with milling plane at the lower end of the intermediate shaft.

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
