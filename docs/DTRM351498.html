Power Battery Pack Assembly (TZ200XSAC) - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Power off the high voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of High Voltage Electrical System (TZ200XSAC）

  3. Remove the battery pack bumper beam assembly. See Battery Pack Bumper Beam Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the front floor left mudguard assembly. See Front Floor Left Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front floor right mudguard assembly. See Front Floor Right Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Discharging the refrigerant. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

  7. Remove the wiring harness.

    1. Rotate the connector locking bracket, and disconnect one connector.

    2. Detach 1 fixing clips.

  8. Remove the high voltage wiring harness.

    Warning
    After the high-voltage wiring harness is disconnected, use a special plug to seal the high-voltage battery port and wiring harness end, thus preventing electric leakage or dust.

    1. Remove one fixing bolt, and move away the refrigeration tube.

    2. Remove 2 fixing bolts and pull out the connecting end of the high voltage distribution wiring harness assembly.

  9. Use the battery pack lifting platform to support the power battery, remove 10 fixing bolts, slowly lower and move out the power battery pack assembly.

    Warning

    • Since the high-voltage battery pack is very heavy, operate the battery pack lifting platform in accordance with the operating specifications to avoid tilting or unbalanced placement.

    • Avoid violent removal, falls, collision, or trampling of the high-voltage battery pack.

Installation

Caution

  • In the lifting process, please check that no parts interfering each other exist above the high voltage battery pack to prevent squeezing.

  • Lock the connector firmly.

  • After installation, carry out charging inspection and road test inspection to ensure normal input and output of high voltage battery pack.

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

  2. Refrigerant filling. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant
