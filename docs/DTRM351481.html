Outside Rearview Mirror Left Holder Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Press the inner side of the outside rearview mirror left holder assembly to the limit position.

    Caution

    Press the mirror carefully so as to avoid damage to the rearview mirror glass and the internal structure.

  3. Release 8 clamping jaws.

  4. Disconnect 2 connectors, and take out the outside rearview mirror left holder assembly.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
