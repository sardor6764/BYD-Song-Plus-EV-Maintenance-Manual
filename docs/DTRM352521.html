Right Front Door Wiring Harness - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the right front door inner snap handle cover. See Right Front Door Inner Snap Handle Cover - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the right front door inner handle cover. See Right Front Door Inner Handle Cover - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the right front door guard with accessory assembly. See Right Front Door Guard with Accessory Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the right front door inner waist belt. See Right Front Door Inner Waist Belt - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the right front door glass assembly. See Right Front Door Glass Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the right front door bracket assembly. See Right Front Door Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the right front door module. See Right Front Door Module - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the instrument panel right end guard assembly. See Instrument Panel Right End Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the right front door sill guard assembly. See Right Front Door Sill Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  11. Disconnect one connector.

  12. Disconnect one connector.

  13. Disconnect one connector.

  14. Pry up 3 fixing clips.

  15. Pry up 10 fixing clips.

  16. Disconnect one connector.

  17. Pry up 1 fixing clips.

  18. Disconnect one connector.

  19. Disconnect one connector.

  20. Disconnect one connector.

  21. Pry up 1 fixing clips.

  22. Detach one through-hole rubber sleeve.

  23. Detach 1 through-hole rubber sleeves, and take out right front door wiring harness.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
