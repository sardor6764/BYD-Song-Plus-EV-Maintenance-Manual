Service Method

Vehicle service plan

The vehicle service shall be carried out as planned to ensure stable, safe and economic driving, and reduce fault. Generally, the service is carried out according to the schedule, depending on the mileage or time interval, whichever comes first. For the service items that have exceeded the deadline, they shall also be done at the same time interval.

If the vehicle mainly operates under one or more of the following special conditions, some service items need to be carried out more frequently.

Road conditions

  • Driving on rough, muddy or snowmelt roads.

  • Driving on dusty roads.

Driving conditions

  • Towing a trailer, using a camping trailer or roof carrier.

  • Frequent short distance driving (＜8km) and the outside temperature below 0°C.

  • Long term idling and/or long-distance driving at low speed, such as police cars, taxis or vehicles transporting goods.
