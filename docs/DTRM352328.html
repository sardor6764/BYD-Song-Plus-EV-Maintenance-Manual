Rear Bumper Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the left rear light guard assembly. See Left Rear Light Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the right rear light guard assembly. See Right Rear Light Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the left rear wheel eyebrow trim panel assembly. See Left Rear Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the right rear wheel eyebrow trim panel assembly. See Right Rear Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove 3 fixing screws.

  7. Remove 3 fixing screws.

  8. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 5±1N•m

  9. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 5±1N•m

  10. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 5±1N•m

  11. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 5±1N•m

  12. Lift the vehicle, and disconnect one connector.

  13. Detach 1 fixing clips.

  14. Remove 4 fixing clips.

  15. Move out the rear bumper assembly.

    Reminder

    Pull out the fitting parts between both ends of the bumper and the rear bumper left and right bracket assemblies, and two people pull the rear bumper outward for a short distance from both sides in the middle until the rear bumper flange detaches from the left and right combination rear light.

  16. Disconnect one connector, and take out the rear bumper assembly.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
