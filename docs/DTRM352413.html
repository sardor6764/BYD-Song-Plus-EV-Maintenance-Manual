Damper Control Motor - Removal and Installation

Reminder
The removal and installation methods of the (mode) damper control motor and the (heating-cooling) damper control motor are the same. Take the (mode) damper control motor as an example.

Removal

  1. Refrigerant recovery. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

  2. Remove the pipe beam welding assembly components. See Pipe Beam Welding Assembly Components - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the channel temperature sensor (front passenger floor duct assembly). See Channel Temperature Sensor (Front Passenger Floor Duct Assembly) - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the channel temperature sensor (driver's side floor duct assembly). See Channel Temperature Sensor (Driver's Side Floor Duct Assembly) - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front passenger floor duct assembly. See Front Passenger's Side Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the driver's side floor duct assembly. See Driver's Side Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the HVAC assembly. See HVAC Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the internal and external circulation motor. See Internal and External Circulation Motor - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the filter cover. See Filter Cover - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the high efficiency filter. See High Efficiency Filter - Removal and Installation

  11. Remove the air inlet assembly. See Air Inlet Assembly - Removal and Installation

  12. Remove the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit. See Air Conditioner Mode Linkage Maintenance Kit - Removal and Installation

  13. Disconnect one connector.

  14. Press one clamping jaw, and take out the damper control motor.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
