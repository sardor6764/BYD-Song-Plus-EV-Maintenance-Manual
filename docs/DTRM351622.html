Discharge and filling brake fluid

Operating procedures

  1. Remove the brake fluid reservoir cover.

  2. Take out the filter.

  3. Drain old brake fluid from the brake fluid reservoir.

    Caution
    Keep a small amount of brake fluid in the brake fluid reservoir to prevent air from entering the brake line.

  4. Remove the left rear wheel assembly. See Wheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Open the air vent dust cover.

  6. Connect the transparent hose of the exhaust cylinder to the air vent.

  7. Loosen the vent screw, and the assistant press down the brake pedal to the bottom until no brake fluid flows out, and then tighten the vent screw and release the brake pedal.

  8. Repeat the above step until clear brake fluid flows out.

    Caution
    When discharging the brake fluid, add brake fluid to the brake fluid reservoir continuously to prevent the brake fluid level from falling below “MIN”.

  9. Pull out the transparent hose (brake fluid collection bottle end), and place the transparent hose in a clean container, loosen the vent screw, collect a small amount of brake fluid, and tighten the vent screw.

  10. Measure the water content of the brake fluid with a brake fluid tester. If the brake fluid contains no water, indicate the brake fluid change completes.

  11. Connect the transparent hose to the brake fluid collection bottle.

  12. Press the brake pedal for several times continuously, hold the brake pedal in the pressed state, loosen the vent screw, and observe, and then tighten the vent screw when no bubble coming out in the bottle.

  13. Repeat the above step 3 ~ 4 times to ensure there is no air in the brake system.

  14. Pull out the transparent hose, take out the wrench, and install the air vent dust cover.

  15. Install the left rear wheel assembly. See Wheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  16. Repeat above operation for the left rear, the left front, the right front and the right rear wheels.

  17. Drain excess brake fluid from the brake fluid reservoir, make sure the brake fluid level reaches the “MAX” marker on the brake fluid reservoir.

  18. Clean up spilled brake fluid.

  19. Install the filter.

  20. Install the brake fluid reservoir cover.
