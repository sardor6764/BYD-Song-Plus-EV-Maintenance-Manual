Battery Safety

Risky Vehicles

The power battery is at risk in the following cases:

  1. The vehicle has been wading for more than 30min, and the water depth reaches the door sill, but there is no water in the vehicle.

  2. The vehicle power battery high voltage and low voltage connectors are immersed in water.

  3. The power battery is deformed, punctured and leaked after an impact.

  4. The vehicle is on fire.

Risk Determination

  1. First detect the highest temperature point of the power battery with a handheld infrared thermal imager or infrared thermometer when a risky vehicle is transported to or enters the store.

    Caution
    Handheld infrared thermal imager/infrared temperature tester: the range must cover -50°C ~ 380°C.

  2. The temperature of the damaged part of the power battery pack is 10°C higher than the ambient temperature or there is a significant temperature rise relative to the ambient temperature (2°C/min).

    Caution
    spray a large amount of water to cool the battery pack until the temperature drops below the ambient temperature.

  3. The battery tray is severely deformed (the dent-depth of the bearing area is more than 10mm) and damaged.

  4. The battery has been ablated by flame or baked for a long time (flame distance ＞ 10cm, duration ＞ 10min).

  5. The battery is ablated by flame, with ablation symptom, and baked at close range (flame distance ＜ 10cm, duration ＞ 3min).

  6. An electric leakage fault is reported after wading for over 30min.

  7. The battery tray is damaged or leaking.

  8. Vehicles that meet any of the above conditions are high-risk vehicles.

Transportation of Risky Vehicles

For vehicles that need to be transported to the store for maintenance, determine the risk level according to the risk identification content, and conduct the pre-transportation treatment accordingly:

  1. For vehicles with risks, use a hand-held infrared thermal imager or infrared thermometer to detect the maximum temperature of the power battery. Vehicles with no abnormal temperature and no high risk can be transported to the store for further overhaul.

  2. For vehicles with abnormal temperature or high risks, spray a large amount of water to cool the battery pack. After the fire is extinguished, continuously cool it with water for more than 30min until the temperature drops below the ambient temperature, and then it can be transported.

  3. For the power battery with abnormal temperature during transportation, re-measure the temperature every 5min to determine whether cooling is required. For high-risk vehicles without abnormal temperature, re-measure the temperature every 10min during transportation to determine whether cooling is required.

    Caution
    If possible, use a temperature monitoring system to monitor the temperature of the battery pack, where the monitoring points shall not be less than 8.

Disposal of Risky Vehicles

When a risky vehicle arrives, determine the risk level according to the risk identification content, and conduct the treatment accordingly:

  1. For vehicles with risks, use a hand-held infrared thermal imager or infrared thermometer to detect the maximum temperature of the power battery. If the temperature is normal and the vehicle is not at high risk, overhaul the vehicle according to the "Safe Maintenance" chapter and the Maintenance Manual, but the battery temperature must be detected every 1h. If there is no exception for 12h, the battery will be deemed as not at risk.

  2. For vehicles with abnormal temperature or high risk, the battery pack must be cooled by spraying a large amount of water until the temperature drops below the ambient temperature. The specific operations are as below:

    1. When a vehicle with abnormal temperature arrives, first cool it down (at the same time, cut off the low voltage power of vehicle). The next operation shall be carried out after its temperature is below the ambient temperature.

    2. When a vehicle with abnormal temperature cools down, remove the battery pack according to the "Safe Maintenance” chapter. For high-risk vehicles, remove the battery pack in the first place. Monitor the power battery temperature throughout the process. For abnormal temperature rise, use a large amount of water to cool it down.

    3. Transfer the removed power battery to a safe area. Remove the sealing cover (use a knife to cut the adhesive sealing cover and avoid scratching the personnel). Quickly disconnect the connecting pieces and BIC connectors between modules with an electric insulating tool.

    4. Take out the deformed and heating modules separately and place them in 3.6% salt water. A large amount of hydrogen and oxygen will be produced by electrolysis. Keep away from fire source and well ventilated. Never wear any metal ornaments to avoid sparks. Avoid static electricity in dry environment. Spraying water is acceptable. After being placed in 3.6% salt water for 12h, ensure the module voltage is lower than 1/2 of the nominal voltage. Dry the modules with an air gun. And then move them to the high-risk battery storage area.

    5. In case of water immersion, damage and heating of the sampling line, open the side cover of each module and cut off all core sampling lines (cut off 1cm close to the core to prevent re-contact). This is only applicable to the battery where the centralized BMS uses the wire as the sampling line. This is not applicable to the battery where the distributed BMS uses the FPC sampling line.

    6. After the battery is removed, overhaul the vehicle according to the "Safe Maintenance" chapter and the Maintenance Manual.

Storage Conditions of High-risk Battery

  1. Discharge and dry the high-risk power batteries. Power batteries in different risks shall be stored in different categories. Relevant information signs (vehicle information, fault conditions, etc.) shall be made. The leaked batteries shall be stored after anti-corrosion and anti-leakage packaging.

  2. The storage site is a separate area for hazardous power batteries, which shall be far away from the production area. Warning signs such as high voltage and danger shall be made. Other materials or personnel are not allowed to approach. The site shall be equipped with basic monitoring facilities for regular patrol inspection.

  3. Stacking is not allowed. Multi-layer steel shelves can be used (the weighting capacity of each layer shall be greater than twice the capacity of the stored battery, and the shelves shall have clear identification of the weighting capacity). At the same time, the power battery shall be placed horizontally, and shall not be placed obliquely, or upside down. Other items shall not be stacked on the power batteries to avoid damage caused by compression.

  4. The storage environment shall be well ventilated and dry, free from direct sunlight, exposure or rain, and protected against dust, water and static electricity. The storage temperature of the power battery should be controlled at -20~45°C and the humidity should be 5~95%.

  5. The storage area shall be provided with smoke alarm, automatic sprinkler system, automatic smoke exhaust system and other fire extinguishing facilities (infrared temperature monitoring system is recommended to realize remote alarm).

Transportation Conditions

  1. The high-risk power batteries to be transported must have no abnormal temperature rise within 24h after discharge and drying.

  2. Protective packaging (waterproof, moisture-proof, anti-static and anti-corrosion) shall be used.

  3. Loading environment.

    1. Ensure the roads are clear. There shall be no direct sunlight, inflammable, explosive and hazardous chemicals within the range of loading activities. Never let water and other liquids enter the power battery. In case of bad weather such as rain, suspend the outdoor loading.

  4. Requirements for handling equipment.

    1. Use professional equipment and tools, such as electric forklift and manual forklift with appropriate capacity, to handle and place the power batteries to avoid damage.

  5. Requirements for carrier vehicles.

    1. The high-risk power batteries shall be transported in a closed carriage to prevent water from entering. Power batteries shall be transported separately. If other items need to be loaded on the same carrier vehicle, liquid, flammable, explosive and hazardous chemicals are not allowed.

  6. Requirements for handling.

    1. When using the equipment to handle the power batteries, professional personnel shall operate the equipment and handle it with care. Avoid falling, rolling and heavy pressure. The packed power batteries shall be loaded in the warehouse. Never load power batteries halfway or stack other items on them.

  7. Requirements for transportation process.

    1. Land transportation: avoid squeezing and impact between power batteries or between power batteries and carrier vehicle body due to sudden braking. The storage temperature of the power batteries shall be -20°C~45°C and the humidity shall be 5~95%. The transport time shall be within 72h. If not, conduct special treatment to control the temperature and humidity to ensure that the products are not drenched or exposed to the sun.

    2. Sea transportation and air transportation: apply special packaging with hazardous packaging certification. Ordinary packaging is not allowed. MH/T1020-2009 shall be followed and UN38.3 test shall be passed for air transportation. The ambient temperature around the packaging boxes shall not exceed 45°C, and the products must not be drenched or exposed to the sun.

  8. Handling of land transportation emergencies.

    1. In case of leakage of power battery, stop the transportation and contact after-sales personnel of BYD. If the electrolyte of the power battery splashes on the skin and eyes, immediately wash it with plenty of water for more than 15min, and then seek medical advice.

    2. When the power battery is on fire, use a non-water-based fire extinguisher to put out it. If the fire is large, use a large amount of water to extinguish the fire. After the fire is extinguished, keep cooling it with water for 30min.

    3. If the packaging boxes are accidentally drenched, immediately remove them, to prevent the wet boxes from becoming conductors, causing power battery discharge or burning power battery terminals.

    4. In case of impact and serious extrusion during transportation, stop the transportation. The transportation can be continued only after the professionals eliminate the safety hazards.

  9. Requirements for unloading.

    1. The unloading environment and equipment are consistent with the loading. The unloading place shall be protected from direct sunlight and at least 2m away from heat source. Before unloading, check whether the packaging boxes are damaged on the carrier vehicle. Note the height of the carriage to prevent the battery damage caused by the impact between the forklift and the carriage.
