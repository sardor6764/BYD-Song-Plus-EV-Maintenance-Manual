Front Passenger Seat Assembly (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the front passenger seat left front foot protector (electric seat). See Front Passenger Seat Left Front Foot Protector (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the front passenger seat right front foot protector (electric seat). See Front Passenger Seat Right Front Foot Protector (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the front passenger seat left rear foot protector (electric seat). See Front Passenger Seat Left Rear Foot Protector (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the front passenger seat right rear foot protector (electric seat). See Front Passenger Seat Right Rear Foot Protector (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front passenger seat belt lower anchor point end cap (electric seat). See Front Passenger Seat Belt Lower Anchor Point End Cap (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 45N • m

  7. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    Caution
    Adjust the front passenger seat assembly (electric seat) to the rearmost position.

    • Tightening torque: 45N • m

  8. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    Caution
    Adjust the front passenger seat assembly (Electric Seat) to the frontmost position.

    • Tightening torque: 45N • m

  9. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  10. Disconnect 3 connectors ①, pry up one fixing clip ②, and take out the front passenger seat assembly (Electric Seat).

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
