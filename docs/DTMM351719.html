System Function Principle

  1. The multimedia system includes 4G network, PAD, Bluetooth phone, intelligent voice recognition, EHU interaction, vehicle radio, USB, SD card assembly, audio&video equipment interface and others.

  2. Main components:

    1. Host assembly.

    2. PAD display assembly.

    3. USB interface assembly.

    4. 4GWiFiBT antenna.

    5. Power amplifier assembly.

    6. Speakers.

    7. Noise reduction MIC.

    8. Digital video recorder image module.

    9. Car U-segment microphone.

  3. USB interface assembly:

    1. 2 USB interfaces, of which only one supports audio and video file reading, and both USB interfaces can charge handheld equipment.

  4. MP3/WMA file playback:

    1. Playable MP3 files.

    2. Playable WMA files.

  5. 4GWiFiBT antenna

    1. 4G Internet access, WiFi connection and others.

  6. Radio:

    1. Radio band: FM:87.5mhz~108.0MHz.

    2. Radio reception problem.

      Caution
      In addition to stereo, there are other problems such as "phase modulation", "multiplexing", and "sound attenuation” which are not caused by electronic noise, but the transmission and reception of the signals.

    3. Phase modulation: AM broadcasting is easily subject to electromagnetic or other interference, so phase modulation occurs. Phase modulation usually occurs at night. The vehicle receives two signals from the same emitter, one is reflected by the ionosphere, and the other is directly received from the emitter.

    4. Multi-channel interference: this is caused by the vehicle receiving two signals from the same emitter, where one is received from the emitter, and the other is reflected by buildings, mountains or other obstacles.

    5. Signal attenuation: this is caused by the reflection deviation of some signals by huge obstacles (buildings, mountains, etc.) between the emitter and the receiving radio, resulting in reduced signal wave energy. High frequency signals are easier to be blocked.
