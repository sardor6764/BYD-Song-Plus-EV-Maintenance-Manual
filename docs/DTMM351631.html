System Description

  • A module installed inside the tire to measure tire pressure and transmit information wirelessly.

  • It is installed at and integrated with the tire valve, which is divided into the monitoring module part and the valve part.

System Function Principle

The tire pressure monitoring system uses the tire pressure monitoring module installed in each tire to measure the tire pressure, acceleration and other parameters. Through radio frequency transmission, the collected data is sent to the tire pressure monitoring control module, which demodulates, analyzes and processes the radio frequency data into CAN signals, and sends the CAN signals to the instrument for display. In such way, the tire related information is digitally displayed in real time. When the pressure of any tire exceeds the threshold, the system will make sound, light and vision alarms to remind the driver so as to ensure driving safety.
