Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • Avoid contact of liquid refrigerant with eyes and skin. If liquid refrigerant contacts eyes and skin, rinse with cold water and never rub eyes or skin. Apply Vaseline jelly on the affected skin. For serious cases, seek medical advice immediately.

  • The total amount of refrigerant oil is 120mL. When the total amount of refrigerant oil is less than 110mL due to leakage, it may cause excessive wear of the compressor. The maintenance station shall add refrigerant oil as appropriate.

  • During maintenance, replace the "O" ring of the opened pipeline, apply refrigerant oil on the seal before assembly, and then connect it to according to the required torque.

  • Follow the technical requirements (filling amount, model of refrigerant oil, torque requirements, etc.). Overhaul the air conditioner as required to ensure the normal operation and service life of the air conditioning system.

  • The refrigerant oil is easy to absorb water. When removing the refrigeration pipeline, immediately plug or cover the pipe orifice to prevent moisture or dust from entering the refrigeration system.

  • When the electric compressor assembly is running, never enable the high pressure valve of the pressure gauge, but only enable and disable the low pressure valve.

  • Never discharge refrigerant too quickly to avoid pumping out the refrigerant oil.

  • Avoid excessive refrigerant. Excessive refrigerant will lead to poor refrigeration.

Warning

  • The working area shall be ventilated before the maintenance. Never operate the refrigerant in an enclosed space or near an open fire. Wear goggles before the maintenance and keep them until maintenance is completed.

  • Never operate the electric compressor assembly if there is not enough refrigerant in the refrigeration system. Avoid burnout of the electric compressor assembly due to insufficient refrigerant and insufficient oil lubrication in the system.

  • Special refrigerant oil must be used. Never use other brands of lubricating oil, let alone mix them (different brands).

  • Before the removal, power off the high voltage electrical system.
