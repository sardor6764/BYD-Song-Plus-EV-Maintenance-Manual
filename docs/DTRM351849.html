Inspection of Tire and Inflation Pressure

Reminder
四个车轮的检查方法一致，下面以左前侧为例。

  1. Check the surface of tires.

    • Check whether the tires are leaky, cracks and bulges on the surface.

    • Check whether the tire has abnormal wear.

    • Check whether the tire is worn to the wear mark. If so, replace the tire. See Radial Tire - Removal and Installation

  2. Check whether the tire pressure is 2.5bar by tire gauge.
