Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution
Important notes on starter iron battery:

  • Regularly check the surface of the starter iron battery and the positive and negative terminals to ensure that they are not loose or rusted.

  • The scrapped starter iron battery shall be treated in a centralized manner. Recycle the starter iron battery according to local laws and regulations to prevent harm to the environment.

  • When replacing the starter iron battery, use the starter iron battery of the same model and specification.

  • Avoid overcharging or long-term power loss of starter iron battery.

  • Avoid long-term large current discharge of starter iron battery.

  • Install the starter iron battery properly on the vehicle to avoid vibration.

  • Clean up the oxide and sulfate produced by the fixing clips of the terminals of the starter iron battery, and apply grease to avoid re corrosion.

  • Never charge the starter iron battery separately.

  • The starter iron battery works normally only after communicating with the vehicle. Correctly connect the connector and wiring harness.

  • When cleaning the starter iron battery, avoid liquid entering the starter iron battery.

  • The starter iron battery has a built-in relay. It is normal for the relay to make a sound when starting the vehicle.

  • Avoid bumping the starter iron battery. Once the starter iron battery is cracked, stop using it and properly recycle it.

  • Before disconnecting the negative pole of the starter iron battery, turn off all the consumers and set the power state to OFF.

  • When maintaining the supplemental restraint system, disconnect the negative pole of the starter iron battery and wait for at least 90s before the next step.

Warning

Important warnings on starter iron battery:

  • When removing the starter iron battery terminals, remove the negative terminal first. If the positive terminal is removed first and contacts any metal in the surrounding area, sparks may be generated, causing fire, electric shock, serious injury and even life-threatening.

  • Turn off all consumers on the vehicle before connecting the starter iron battery again. First connect the positive terminal, and then the negative terminal. Never install the starter iron battery in the wrong order. Otherwise, there is a risk of fire.

  • Keep the starter iron battery away from heat source and open fire. Keep ventilation during charging and use. Otherwise, fire or personal injury may be caused.

  • The starter iron battery has a built-in power manager. Never disassemble or maintain the starter iron battery without permission to avoid damaging the starter iron battery or causing personal injury.
