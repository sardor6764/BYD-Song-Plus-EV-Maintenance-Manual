Subframe Sub-Assembly with Front Drive Assembly Accessory (TZ200XSAC)

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Power off the high voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of High Voltage Electrical System (TZ200XSAC）

  3. Discharge the coolant. See Coolant Discharging and Filling

  4. Refrigerant recovery. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

  5. Replacement of transmission gear oil. See Replacement of Transmission Gear Oil

  6. Remove universal joint dust cover assembly I. See Universal Joint Dust Cover Assembly I - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the left front drive half shaft assembly. See Left Front Drive Half Shaft Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the right front drive half shaft assembly. See Right Front Drive Half Shaft Assembly - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the electric compressor bracket assembly. See Electric Compressor Bracket - Removal and Installation (TZ200XSAC)

  10. Remove the wiring harness.

    1. Disconnect 3 connectors.

    2. Remove one fixing nut.

  11. Disconnect 4 fixing clips.

  12. Remove the end cover of integrated intelligent front drive assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    2. Remove 4 fixing bolts.

  13. Remove 5 fixing bolts.

    Caution
    Once the contact of high voltage wiring harness is disconnected, insulation and sealing treatment should be completed as soon as possible to prevent accidental contact of skin or conductive objects with the terminal, which can cause personal injury or vehicle damage

    • Tightening torque: 9±1N•m

  14. Disconnect one connector.

    Caution

    Once the connector is disconnected, insulation and sealing treatment should be completed as soon as possible to prevent accidental contact of skin or conductive objects with the terminal, which can cause personal injury or vehicle damage.

  15. Detach 2 fixing clips.

  16. Move away the cooling pipeline.

    1. Detach 1 fixing clips.

    2. Remove one elastic clamp, and detach the cooling pipe assembly 4 from the integrated intelligent front drive assembly.

      Caution
      Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

    3. Remove one elastic clamp, and detach the motor coolant pump inlet pipe assembly from the integrated intelligent front drive assembly.

      Caution
      Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

    4. Remove one elastic clamp, and detach the cooling pipe assembly 1 from the integrated intelligent front drive assembly.

      Caution
      Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

  17. Remove one elastic clamp, and detach the cooling pipe assembly 3 from the integrated intelligent front drive assembly.

    Caution
    Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

  18. Remove one fixing bolt.

  19. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

  20. Remove one fixing bolt, and detach the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly from the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly.

  21. Remove one fixing nut, and detach the front stabilizer bar tie rod ball joint assembly from the front stabilizer bar tie rod fitting assembly.

  22. Remove one fixing nut, and detach the front stabilizer bar tie rod ball joint assembly from the front stabilizer bar tie rod fitting assembly.

  23. Remove the front subframe left front mounting bracket assembly. SeeFront Subframe Front Mounting Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  24. Remove the front subframe right front mounting bracket assembly. SeeFront Subframe Front Mounting Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  25. Remove the front subframe left rear mounting bracket assembly. SeeFront Subframe Left Rear Mounting Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  26. Remove the front subframe right rear mounting bracket assembly. SeeFront Subframe Right Rear Mounting Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  27. Using the lifting platform support for subframe sub-assembly with front drive assembly accessory, slowly lower and move the subframe sub-assembly with front drive assembly accessory.

    Caution

    The subframe sub-assembly with front drive assembly accessory is very heavy. Use the lifting platform according to operating specifications to avoid tilting or unbalanced placement.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

  2. Filling the transmission gear oil. See Replacement of Transmission Gear Oil

  3. Filling the coolant. SeeCoolant Discharging and Filling

  4. Test of cooling system pressure. See Test of Cooling System Pressure

  5. Refrigerant filling. SeeRecovery and Filling of Refrigerant
