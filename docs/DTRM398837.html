EPB Applied and Released for Maintenance

Caution

  • Before removing and installing the rear brake calipers and the rear brake pad, execute EPB maintenance release first. It may increase the assemble difficulty and result in hardware damage due to accidental EPB activation

  • After the EPB maintenance release, conduct the EPB maintenance pull-up to exit the maintenance release status. The EPB system will return to normal and respond to all functions.

EPB Released for Maintenance

Caution

Use method 1 and method 2 to complete maintenance release. All functions of EPB will be invalid, so as to avoid accidental action during the maintenance process and result in EPB drive assembly damage. After the maintenance is completed and the vehicle is restored to the normal state, execute "Maintenance Apply" to restore the EPB system to the normal state.

  1. Method 1: Use a VDS perform EPB maintenance release.

    1. Connect a VDS to OBD data link connector.

    2. Power on the vehicle and complete the self-test.

    3. Establish the communication between the VDS and the vehicle.

    4. Tap “Vehicle Diagnosis System”.

    5. Tap "Passenger Car".

    6. Tap "EV Series".

    7. Tap "Song PLUS EV".

    8. Tap “EPB” or “Rear Domain Control module”.

    9. Tap “Action test”.

    10. Tap “Maintenance Mode Release”.

  2. Method 2: Use the EPB switch and follow the steps below to perform the EPB maintenance release.

    1. Set the START/STOP button to ON or start the vehicle.

    2. Press and hold the brake pedal, and the effect shall be “brake light keeps on steadily”.

      Caution

      Press the brake pedal throughout the whole process.

    3. Press and hold EPB switch for over 10 seconds. The alarm indicator (red exclamation point indicator) on the instrument panel will start flashing.

      Caution

      If the holding time is less than 10 seconds or more than 12 seconds, the system will automatically return to the initial state, and repeated operation is required.

    4. Release EPB switch within 2 seconds after the alarm indicator start flashing.

    5. Start timing after releasing the EPB switch, and press and release EPB switch within 3~5 seconds.

      Caution

      Press the switch again within 3 seconds or after 5 seconds after the first release, the system will automatically restore to the original state, and repeated operation is required.

    6. EPB will be fully released, and the alarm indicator keeps on steadily.

      Caution

      After full release, the system will not respond to any function in this case, executing “maintenance apply” or pressing the EPB switch is required to exit the maintenance release state.

    7. Release the brake pedal, operation completed.

  3. Method 3: If both the above two methods fail due to electrical appliance failure, perform the mechanical maintenance release, and the method is as follows.

    1. Remove the EPB motor drive assembly. SeeEPB Motor Drive Assembly - Removal and Installation

    2. Use the socket head wrench to release the brake piston by turning the splines on the brake caliper body clockwise, loosen the brake piston until the thrust screw of the brake caliper body rotates freely (torque≤0.2Nm).

      Caution

      The axial force shall not exceed 10N.

EPB Applied for Maintenance

  1. Method 1: Use a VDS to perform the EPB maintenance pull-up and exit the maintenance release state.

    1. Connect a VDS to OBD data link connector.

    2. Power on the vehicle and complete the self-test.

    3. Establish the communication between the VDS and the vehicle.

    4. Tap “Vehicle Diagnosis System”.

    5. Tap "Passenger Car".

    6. Tap "EV Series".

    7. Tap "Song PLUS EV".

    8. Tap “EPB” or “Rear Domain Control module”.

    9. Tap “Action test”.

    10. Tap “Maintenance Mode Clamping”.

  2. Method 2: Use the EPB switch to enable the EPB maintenance pull-up as below to exit the maintenance release status.

    1. Set the START/STOP button to ON or start the vehicle.

    2. Press and hold the brake pedal, and the brake lights should stay on.

      Caution

      Press the brake pedal throughout the whole process.

    3. Keep pulling up the EPB switch for more than 10 seconds, and the alarm indicator (red exclamation mark) on the instrument will flash.

      Caution

      Keep pressing the switch for more than 10 seconds. Otherwise the system will return to the initial state.

    4. Release the EPB switch, and press down the EPB switch once again within 5 s;

      Caution

      The switch shall be pressed again within 5 second after being released. Otherwise the system will be restored to the initial state.

    5. EPB will be applied, the alarm indicator goes out, and EPB state light lights up.

      Caution

      After pressing the switch to exit the maintenance release state, the EPB system will resume to the normal state, and will be able to respond to all functions.

    6. Release the brake pedal, operation completed.
