Replacement of Transmission Gear Oil

Oil Replacement

  1. Wait for 10 minutes and then raise the vehicle to make it level.

  2. Lift the vehicle. See Lifting Vehicle

  3. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Front Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Place a 5L measuring cup in advance and remove the oil fill plug and sealing gasket.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2N•m

    Caution

    Stay away from components that are hot to prevent burns.

  5. Remove the oil drain plug and sealing gasket.

    • Tightening torque: 50±3N•m

    Reminder

    • Stay away from components that are hot to prevent burns.

    • Use gear oil recycling equipment to collect waste gear oil and follow the local rules and regulations when disposing of it.

  6. When the drained oil changes from columnar shape to linear drop shape, turn the two front wheels clockwise for 2 minutes, and then turn them counterclockwise for 1 minute. Make the front left and right wheels rotate synchronously in the same direction until there is no oil drop.

  7. Replace the filter element assembly (if required). SeeFilter Element Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Install a new oil drain plug and sealing gasket.

    • Tightening torque: 50±3N•m

  9. Add a specific amount of lubricating oil using a gear oil filling machine.

    • Gear oil specification: Castrol BOT383.

    • Gear oil filling amount: 1.5L.

  10. Install the oil fill plug and sealing gasket.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2N•m

  11. Check the transmission gear oil level. SeeInspection of transmission gear oil

  12. Clean any leftover oil on the electric drive assembly after filling the oil.

  13. Install the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Front Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  14. Lower the vehicle.
