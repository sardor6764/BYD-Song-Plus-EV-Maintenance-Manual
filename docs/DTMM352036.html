Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • The integrated power brake assembly can only be replaced as a whole. Partition test or replacement/interchange is not allowed.

  • The brake fluid is corrosive. Never stain the brake fluid on other parts of the vehicle.

  • After the brake tube connector nut is removed, block the sealing pipeline, integrated power brake assembly and other interfaces to prevent foreign matters from entering.

  • The oil pipe joint nut is easy to be misaligned. Screw 3~5 threads into the hole by hand before using an oil pipe wrench.

Warning

  • Before diagnosing the integrated power brake, if there is a fault in the basic brake system, it must be eliminated first, such as noise in the brake system, soft brake pedal, vibration of the brake pedal during normal braking, vehicle braking deviation, and parking brake system fault.

  • The brake fluid is corrosive. Never contaminate the brake fluid on the skin. If so, immediately clean it with plenty of water.

  • The integrated power brake must be maintained by technicians who have received professional trainings and mastered maintenance skills. And only original parts can be used for replacement.

  • When replacing the brake tube, always replace the brake line clamp as well.

  • After the maintenance of the integrated power brake, check it carefully and conduct a road test. The vehicle can be handed over to the customer only after ensuring everything is normal.
