SRS Electronic Control Unit - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the console body assembly components. See Console Body Assembly Components - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the console middle mounting bracket assembly. See Console Middle Mounting Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the floor duct assembly. See Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 8±1N•m

  6. Disconnect one connector.

  7. Remove 3 fixing nuts, and take out the SRS electronic control unit.

    • Tightening torque: 9N • m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
