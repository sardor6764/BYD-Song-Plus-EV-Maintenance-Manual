Evaporator Box Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the HVAC assembly. See HVAC Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the evaporator box air vent transition duct assembly. See Evaporator Box Air Vent Transition Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the rear duct assembly. See Rear Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the driver's side heating-cooling motor. See Driver's Side Heating-Cooling Motor - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the internal and external circulation motor. See Internal and External Circulation Motor - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the filter cover. See Filter Cover - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the high efficiency filter. See High Efficiency Filter - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the air inlet assembly. See Air Inlet Assembly - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit. See Air Conditioner Mode Linkage Maintenance Kit - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the (mode) damper control motor. See Damper Control Motor - Removal and Installation

  11. Remove the (heating-cooling) damper control motor. See Damper Control Motor - Removal and Installation

  12. Remove the integrated PTC air heater assembly. See Integrated PTC Air Heater Assembly - Removal and Installation

  13. Remove the expansion valve. See Expansion Valve - Removal and Installation

  14. Remove the TXV outlet sponge. See Removal of TXV Outlet Sponge

  15. Remove the air conditioning wiring harness. See Air Conditioning Wiring Harness - Removal and Installation

  16. Remove the heater core assembly. See Heater Core Assembly - Removal and Installation

  17. Remove one fixing screw.

  18. Remove one fixing screw.

  19. Remove 3 fixing screws.

  20. Detach 2 clamping jaws.

  21. Detach 1 clamping jaws.

  22. Remove 3 clamping jaws, and take out the evaporator box assembly.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
