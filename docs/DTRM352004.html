Perform Internal Sensor Calibration

VDS Operation Steps

Caution

  • The vehicle is supported by wheels.

  • Driver alone must sit in the vehicle.

  • The steering wheel is straightened, the front wheels are straight forward.

  • Under normal loading and normal tire pressure condition.

  • Interferences such as closing doors or front compartment cover is not allowed.

  1. Conduct calibration on the hub tester or on a flat road surface. The maximum inclination of the calibration bench (or ground) must be within ± 0.57 ° (± 1% slope) (both directions must meet the requirements).

  2. Power on the vehicle. Connect a VDS. And conduct internal sensor calibration.

    1. Tap "Passenger Car".

    2. Tap "EV Series".

    3. Tap "Song PLUS EV".

    4. Tap "ECU Scan"

    5. Tap "EHB (Bosch)".

    6. Tap "Calibration".

    7. Tap "End Line Calibration".

    8. Tap "Internal Sensor Calibration".
