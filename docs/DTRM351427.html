Disassembly of Front Shock Absorber Strut Assembly

Caution
The disassembly methods of the left and right front shock absorber strut assemblies are the same. Take the left front shock absorber strut assembly as an example.

Disassembly

  1. Fix the front shock absorber strut assembly on the workbench.

  2. Compress the front shock absorber coil spring with a special tool (tool name: xxxx; material number: xxxx) until the front shock absorber coil spring is separated from the front shock absorber bearing combination. See the Manual of Special Tools

  3. Remove the front shock absorber dust cover.

  4. Remove the lock nut with a special tool (tool name: front and rear shock absorber nut remover; material number xxxx). See the Manual of Special Tools

  5. Remove the upper support combination of the front shock absorber.

  6. Remove the front shock absorber bearing combination.

  7. Remove the front shock absorber dust cover.

  8. Remove the front shock absorber coil spring.

  9. Remove the upper seat combination of the front shock absorber.

  10. Remove the front shock absorber buffer body combination.

  11. Remove the lower cushion of front shock absorber spring.

  12. Take out the front shock absorber damper assembly.

Assembly

Caution

  • Fit the lower end of the coil spring into the groove of the lower cushion of left rear shock absorber spring .

  • Rotate and align the front shock absorber upper support combination.

  1. Follow the disassembly steps in reverse order to assemble.
