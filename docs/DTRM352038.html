Front Compartment Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the front compartment cover insulation. See Front Compartment Cover Insulation - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the front compartment cover lock ring. See Front Compartment Cover Lock Ring - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the front wash line and nozzle assembly I. See Front Wash Line and Nozzle Assembly I - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the front wash line and nozzle assembly II. See Front Wash Line and Nozzle Assembly II - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front wash line sub-assembly II. See Front Wash Line Sub-assembly II - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove 2 fixing clips, and move away front wash line sub-assembly I.

  7. Pry up one elastic plate, and move away the front compartment right gas spring body.

    Caution
    When removing the front compartment right gas spring body, one person lifts the front compartment cover assembly to prevent the same from falling, while the other person gently pries up the elastic plate to a certain height, and pull out the front compartment right gas spring lever, to remove the front compartment right gas spring.

  8. Pry up one elastic plate, and move away the front compartment left gas spring body.

    Caution
    When removing the front compartment left gas spring body, one person lifts the front compartment cover assembly to prevent the same from falling, while the other person gently pries up the elastic plate to a certain height, and pull out the front compartment left gas spring lever, to remove the front compartment left gas spring.

  9. Remove 2 fixing nuts.

    Caution
    The front compartment cover assembly is heavy. An assistant is required when removing the assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 25±3N•m

  10. Remove 2 fixing nuts, and take out the front compartment cover assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 25±3N•m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

  2. Adjust the front compartment cover. See Adjustment of Front Compartment Cover
