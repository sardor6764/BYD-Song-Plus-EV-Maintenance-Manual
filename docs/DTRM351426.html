Right Front Shock Absorber Strut Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the ventilation cover assembly. See Ventilation Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the right front wheel assembly. SeeWheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Detach 3 fixing clips.

  4. Remove one fixing bolt, and move away the brake hose.

    • Tightening torque: 25N • m

  5. Remove one fixing nut, and detach the front stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly from the right front shock absorber strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 90N • m

    Reminder
    To prevent the ball joint from rotating, a socket head wrench can be used to secure it.

  6. Remove 2 fixing nuts.

    • Tightening torque: 230N • m

  7. Remove 3 fixing nuts and take out the right front shock absorber damper assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 75N • m

    Caution
    An assistant is required to support the right front shock absorber strut assembly during removal to prevent it from falling.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
