System Principle

  1. The park assist system is a vehicle monitoring system which imitates the bat flight detection. Several small probes are installed at the rear and front of the vehicle to transmit and receive signals and feed back to the control module. The control module calculates the distance of the measured obstacles based on the signal turn-back time, and then triggers different sound and indicator alarms according to distances, so as to help reversing and parking.

  2. The park assist system is enabled, which meets the working conditions of the probes. The left domain control module controls the front and rear small probes to detect obstacles through ultrasonic waves, makes the buzzer alarm, and sends messages to the multimedia and power amplifier. The multimedia and power amplifier will analyze them and then make sound and image alarms.

  3. When the reversing radar system is enabled, the vehicle speed is less than 10km/h, and the small probe detects obstacles, the buzzer will make an alarm.

  4. The shorter the distance between the vehicle and the obstacle, the more frequent the alarm sound. When the distance between the obstacle and the vehicle is within 0.3m, the buzzer will sound for a long time. Never continue driving at this time.
