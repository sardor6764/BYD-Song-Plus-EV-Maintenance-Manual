Right Rear Door Outer Handle Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the right rear door inner snap handle cover. See Right Rear Door Inner Snap Handle Cover - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the right rear door inner handle cover. See Right Rear Door Inner Handle Cover - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the right rear door guard with accessory assembly. See Right Rear Door Guard with Accessory Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the right rear door bracket assembly. See Right Rear Door Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the right rear door module. See Right Rear Door Module - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the right rear door rear movable guide rail and mud tank assembly. See Right Rear Door Rear Movable Guide Rail and Mud Tank Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the right rear door glass assembly. See Right Rear Door Glass Assembly - Removal and Installation

  9. Detach one fixing clip.

  10. Remove the D20 plastic cover.

  11. Loosen one fixing screw.

  12. Take out the right rear door outer handle.

  13. Take out the fake lock cylinder.

  14. Take out the gasket.

  15. Loosen one fixing screw, and take out the right rear door outer handle seat.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
