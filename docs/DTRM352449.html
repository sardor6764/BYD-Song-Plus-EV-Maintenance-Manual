Air Conditioner Mode Linkage Maintenance Kit - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Refrigerant recovery. See Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

  2. Remove the HVAC assembly. See HVAC Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the internal and external circulation motor. See Internal and External Circulation Motor - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the filter cover. See Filter Cover - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the high efficiency filter. See High Efficiency Filter - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the air inlet assembly. See Air Inlet Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Pry up one clamping jaw, and take out the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit 1.

  8. Pry up one clamping jaw, and take out the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit 1.

  9. Remove the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit 3.

  10. Take out the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit 4.

  11. Take out the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit 5.

  12. Take out the air conditioner mode linkage maintenance kit 6.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
