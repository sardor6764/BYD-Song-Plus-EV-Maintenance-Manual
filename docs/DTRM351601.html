Intelligent Power Brake System Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off and power on the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Power on the vehicle. Connect a VDS. Make the intelligent power brake system assembly enter "installation mode". SeeEnter Installation Mode

  3. Discharge the brake fluid. See Discharge and filling brake fluid

  4. Disconnect 2 connectors.

  5. Remove one elastic clamp, and detach the brake fluid reservoir connecting hose assembly from the intelligent power brake system assembly.

    Caution
    Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

  6. Move away the brake tube assembly.

    1. Remove 2 pipe fixing nuts.

      • Tightening torque: 18±2N•m

    2. Remove 1 pipe fixing nuts.

      • Tightening torque: 20±2N•m

  7. Remove 1 pipe fixing nuts.

    • Tightening torque: 20±2N•m

  8. Remove 4 fixing nuts.

    Caution

    • Prevent the integrated power brake assembly from falling from the front compartment.

    • Prevent the brake pedal assembly bracket from deformation.

    • Avoid damage or deformation of the brake tube.

    • Tightening torque: 25N • m

  9. Push out the ball socket with a special tool (tool name: IPB CV joint removal toolkit; material number: xxxx) and take out the intelligent power brake system assembly from the front compartment. See the Manual of Special Tools

Installation

  1. Hold the motor and auxiliary area of the integrated power brake system assembly for handling.

  2. Hold the mounting plate and auxiliary area of the intelligent power brake system assembly for handling.

  3. Hold the auxiliary area of the intelligent power brake system assembly with one hand, and touch only the green area indicated in the diagram with the other hand.

    Caution
    The green area allows contact for balance, while the red area forbids it.

  4. Connect the IPB push rod and the brake pedal. Make sure the push rod does not tilt more than 3° in any direction after installation.

    Caution

    • Never pull the pedal up after the IPB push rod is connected to the brake pedal, so as to avoid damage to the internal structural parts of the IPB.

    • Never apply lateral force to the IPB push rod when connecting it.

  5. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

    Caution

    • Avoid impacting the intelligent power brake system assembly during installation, and ensure that the assembly's mounting pad and the mounting surface on the body are clear and clean.

    • Gently press the brake pedal surface to press the ball joint of the push rod of the intelligent power brake system into the ball socket of the pedal arm. In the process of connection, avoid pushing the push rod of the intelligent power brake system from the side.

    • Never pull up the brake pedal after the push rod of the intelligent power brake system is connected with the brake pedal, so as to avoid damaging the internal structural parts of the intelligent power brake system.

    • Remove the sealing plug of the outlet of the intelligent power brake system and the brake tube, and avoid damaging the thread and sealing area in this process.

    • Pay attention to installing the brake tube correctly without error. Otherwise, brake failure may occur.

  6. Adjust the brake light switch clearance with a special tool (tool name: brake light switch gap adjustment toolkit; material number: xxxx). See the Manual of Special Tools

  7. Filling the brake fluid. See Discharge and filling brake fluid

  8. Use the dry exhaust method to remove the air from the brake system. See Exhaust of Brake System

  9. Power on the vehicle. Connect a VDS. Make the intelligent power brake system assembly "exit installation mode". SeeExit Installation Mode

  10. Perform vehicle configuration writing. SeeVehicle configuration writing

  11. (VPC) valve body calibration. See(VPC) valve body calibration

  12. Perform internal sensor calibration. SeePerform Internal Sensor Calibration

  13. Switch on the power of vehicle again, and check whether the warning lights of brake system go off.

  14. Clear the DTC of intelligent power brake system. SeeClearing DTCs of Intelligent Power Brake System

  15. Perform a road test.
