Multifunction video controller assembly

System Function Principle

  1. Advantages of integration of front millimeter wave radar and multifunction video controller:

    1. The multifunction video controller assists the front millimeter wave radar to identify the stationary vehicle ahead, and actively decelerates at a suitable distance to avoid impact.

    2. The multifunction video controller assists the front millimeter wave radar to identify the closest forward vehicle as the target vehicle and follow it.

    3. The multifunction video controller and the front millimeter wave radar identify the cut-in vehicles on both sides as the target vehicles, and take braking measures if necessary.

    4. The multifunction video controller and the front millimeter wave radar identify the pedestrians ahead, so as to reduce the possibility of impact.

  2. Functions to be realized for the fusion of front millimeter wave radar and multifunction video controller:

    1. Predictive emergency brake system: including pedestrian collision warning (PCW) and automatic emergency braking (AEB). When the system detects that there is a potential impact risk between the subject vehicle and the forward vehicle, pedestrian or other objects, it will make an audible and visual alarm. When the situation continues to deteriorate, it will apply brake jerk or even automatic emergency braking to avoid impact or reduce the injury.

    2. Traffic sign recognition system: identify the road speed limit signs through the camera sensor. The speed limit indicator icon will turn on to remind the driver.

    3. Intelligent high beam assist system: determine the current driving environment through the camera sensor, and automatically activate or deactivate high beams.

    4. Lane departure warning system: identify lane boundaries through camera sensors. When the vehicle speed is greater than 60km/h and the driver deviates from the lane unconsciously, the system will send an alarm to remind the driver.
