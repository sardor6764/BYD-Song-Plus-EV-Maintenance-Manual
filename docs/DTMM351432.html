Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • To work in the vehicle, cover the seat and other parts to avoid oil stain.

  • Use a interior trim crow plate to remove the interior trim. If there is no interior trim crow plate, use a wrapped screwdriver or equivalent.

  • Since most interior trims are fixed by clips, ensure that all clips have been pried out before removing the interior trims.

  • When installing parts, check whether the fixing clips of parts are missing. If so, add acceptable fixing clips.

  • Check and replace all damaged clips.

  • Place trims in a clean and flat position to avoid damaging the surface.

  • Thoroughly check the parts before the maintenance or replacement.

  • After the installation, check whether the specified torques are applied to the fasteners.

  • When removing and installing the front door assembly, an assistant is required to prevent the front door assembly from falling suddenly, causing personal injury and vehicle damage

  • In case of accidental blocking during the use of the power window, cut off the power of starter iron battery to prevent the window regulator motor from burning out.

  • Wear long sleeved overalls and protective gloves to avoid being cut by the sheet metal parts when removing and installing the interior sheet metal parts of the door.

  • After overhauling the door lock, ensure that the door can be closed only when the door lock is open. Otherwise, the lock may be damaged.

Warning

  • Never use sharp tools to remove the interior trim.

  • Never forcibly remove the interior trim for fear of damage.

  • Never pull the wiring harness when disconnecting the connector for fear of damage to the wiring harness.
