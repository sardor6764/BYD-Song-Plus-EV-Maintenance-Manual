PM2.5 Quick Tester Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the glove box mounting base assembly. See Glove Box Mounting Base Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the ventilation cover assembly. See Ventilation Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Disconnect one connector.

  5. Pry up 3 fixing clips.

  6. Press 4 clamping jaws.

  7. Remove one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 10±1N•m

  8. Remove one fixing nut, and take out the PM2.5 quick tester assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 10N • m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
