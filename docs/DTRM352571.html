Integrated AC/DC Charging Vehicle Socket Sub-Assembly I - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Power off the high voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of High Voltage Electrical System (TZ200XYM）

  3. Remove the battery pack assembly. SeePower Battery Pack Assembly (TZ200XYM) - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the right rear side wall guard assembly. SeeRight Rear Side Wall Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Disconnect one connector.

    Caution

    Once the connector is disconnected, insulation and sealing treatment should be completed as soon as possible to prevent accidental contact of skin or conductive objects with the terminal, which can cause personal injury or vehicle damage.

  6. Remove 4 fixing bolts, and take out the end cover of the integrated intelligent front drive assembly.

  7. Remove the fixing parts of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I in the front compartment.

    1. Remove one fixing bolt.

    2. Remove 4 fixing bolts.

      Caution

      Once the connector is disconnected, insulation and sealing treatment should be completed as soon as possible to prevent accidental contact of skin or conductive objects with the terminal, which can cause personal injury or vehicle damage.

  8. Remove the fixing parts of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I in the front compartment.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    2. Detach 2 fixing clips.

  9. Remove the fixing parts of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I at the body bottom.

    1. Detach 1 fixing clips.

    2. Remove 6 fixing nuts.

  10. Remove the fixing parts of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I at the body bottom.

    1. Detach 7 fixing clips.

    2. Remove 4 fixing nuts.

  11. Remove 1 fixing clips.

  12. Remove the fixing parts of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I at the body bottom.

    1. Detach 2 fixing clips.

    2. Remove 4 fixing nuts.

  13. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

  14. Remove the fixing parts of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I in the rear compartment.

    1. Detach 1 fixing clips.

    2. Remove 6 fixing nuts.

  15. Remove the fixing parts of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I in the rear compartment.

    1. Detach 3 fixing clips.

    2. Disconnect one connector.

    3. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

  16. Remove 4 fixing bolts, and take out the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket sub-assembly I.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
