Carpet Assembly - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the electronic accelerator pedal assembly. See Electronic Accelerator Pedal Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the driver seat left front foot protector. See Driver Seat Left Front Foot Protector - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the driver seat assembly. See Driver Seat Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front passenger seat assembly (electric seat). See Front Passenger Seat Assembly (Electric Seat) - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the console body component. SeeConsole Body Assembly Components - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the floor duct assembly. See Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Remove the curtain assembly. See Cargo Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  9. Remove the trunk lid assembly. See Trunk Lid Assembly - Removal and Installation

  10. Remove the tool box assembly in the middle of the trunk. See Tool Box Assembly in the Middle of the Trunk - Removal and Installation

  11. Remove the rear wall pedal assembly. See Rear Wall Step Assembly - Removal and Installation

  12. Remove trunk lid front end support plate assembly. See Trunk Lid Front End Support Plate Assembly - Removal and Installation

  13. Remove the left rear side wall guard assembly. See Left Rear Side Wall Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  14. Remove the right rear side wall guard assembly. See Right Rear Side Wall Guard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  15. Remove 1 fixing clips.

  16. Remove 1 fixing clips.

  17. Remove 1 fixing clips.

  18. Remove 1 fixing clips.

  19. Remove 1 fixing clips.

  20. Remove one fixing clip, and take out the carpet assembly.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
