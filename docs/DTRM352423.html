Blower - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the glove box assembly. See Glove Box Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the channel temperature sensor (front passenger floor duct assembly). See Channel Temperature Sensor (Front Passenger Floor Duct Assembly) - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the instrument panel right lower sound absorbing baffle assembly. See Instrument Panel Right Lower Sound Absorbing Baffle Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the front passenger floor duct assembly. See Front Passenger's Side Floor Duct Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Disconnect one connector.

  7. Remove one fixing screw.

  8. Remove the blower.

    1. Push the clamping plate.

    2. Take out the blower.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
