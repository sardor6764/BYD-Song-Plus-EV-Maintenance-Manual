Recovery and Filling of Refrigerant

Refrigerant recovering

  1. Remove the front compartment right trim panel assembly. See Front Compartment Right Trim Panel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Turn on the main power switch, “refrigerant weight X.XXKGS” is displayed on the screen. Start the vehicle, turn on the air conditioner to run the electric compressor assembly.

  3. Check the oil level of the drain bottle on the left side of the equipment, and record the oil level. Operate the air conditioner as follows.

  4. Press the “RECOVER” button.

  5. If “XXX KGS has been filtered” is displayed on the screen, it indicates the corresponding amount has been recovered. After replacing with a dry filter, press the start button, the cumulative count will be reset, and the program will return to the menu page.

  6. Connect the high pressure and the lower pressure pipes to the high pressure port and the low pressure port of the on-board air conditioner respectively, open the quick coupling valve, and rotate the high and low pressure valves on the panel anticlockwise to the “RECOVER/VACUUM” position, and press the “Start” button to start recycling.

  7. When recover begins, the equipment will conduct the internal cleaning first, and there are two options: one is default cleaning program (without any changes); and the other is to press “START” to jump out the cleaning program. When measuring the recovery amount is required, select default cleaning program; when quick recovery is required, select jumping out cleaning program.

  8. When the system pressure reaches 25PSI, stop recover automatically.

  9. The equipment will discharge oil automatically, it will take about 90 seconds to fully discharge the oil, record the level increase amount.

  10. On completion, “X. XKGS has been recovered” will be displayed on the screen.

Vacuuming Air Conditioning System

  1. Remove the front compartment right trim panel assembly. See Front Compartment Right Trim Panel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Connect the high pressure and the lower pressure pipes to the high pressure port and the low pressure port of the on-board air conditioner respectively, open the quick coupling valve, and rotate the high and low pressure valves on the panel anticlockwise to the open position.

  3. Press the selection key “RECOVER/VACUUM” on the control panel.

  4. Press the number keys to change the vacuuming time and then press “START”.

  5. During the running process, press “STOP” to suspend the running in case of emergency. Press “START” to resume running; when stop running is required, press “STOP” to exit the program.

  6. After the vacuuming, if refrigerant oil filling is required, execute the following programs; if refrigerant oil filling is not required, press “STOP” to exit the program.

Refrigerant Oil Filling

Caution

The air conditioner must be refilled with refrigerant oil before refilling the refrigerating system of the vehicle, the oil refilling amount depends on the oil discharge amount in the recovery process. The oil discharge amount is the difference of oil level in the plastic bottle on the lower left side of the equipment (the difference before and after the recovery).

  1. Suitable refrigerant oil shall be chosen, wrong refrigerant oil will result in electric compressor piston failure, and reduce the service life.

  2. Pour the oil into the filling bottle.

  3. Close the low-pressure side valve and open the high-pressure side valves clockwise.

  4. Press and hold “F1”, start refrigerant oil filling automatically, and then release “F1”.

  5. After the refrigerant oil filling, press “STOP” to exit the program.

Refrigerant Filling

  1. Remove the front compartment right trim panel assembly. See Front Compartment Right Trim Panel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Connect the high pressure and the lower pressure pipes to the high pressure port and the low pressure port of the on-board air conditioner respectively, open the quick coupling valve, and open the high and low pressure valves on the panel.

  3. Press the function selection key select “CHARGE”.

  4. Press the number keys to change the charging volume, and then press “START” to confirm.

  5. Never interrupt the charging process! When reaching the set value, charging will stop automatically.

  6. During the filling process, always leave a certain amount of refrigerant in the original refrigerant tank, and make sure the refrigerant refilling pipe is connected to the original tank normally.

  7. After reaching the set weight, the equipment will stop charging and prompt “CHARGE COMPLETE", in this case, remove the filling hose.
