Coolant Discharging and Filling

  1. To release the leftover pressure in the cooling system, slowly turn the end cover of the expansion tank assembly I counterclockwise.

  2. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. SeeFront Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove one elastic clamp, detach the cooling pipe assembly 2 from the electric coolant pump assembly I, and drain the coolant.

    Caution
    Waste coolant shall be collected for disposal in accordance with local laws and regulations.

  4. After the coolant is drained, install the connection between the cooling pipe assembly 2 and the electric coolant pump assembly I, and fix it with an elastic clamp.

    Reminder
    Clean up the spilled coolant.

  5. Keep adding coolant to the expansion tank assembly until the level stays at the “MAX” mark.

  6. Power on and let the pump run for about 5 minutes. Add more coolant to the expansion tank at the same time as the liquid level goes down during the operation, until the level stays between “MAX” and “MIN”. Power off and stop the vehicle.

    Caution

    Switch on and off the power for three times, with each circulating coolant pump working for 4 minutes and resting for 1 minute. Keep an eye on the coolant level in the expansion tank and add coolant until it is stable between "MAX" and "MIN".

  7. Install the end cover of the expansion tank assembly I.

  8. Clean the spilled coolant.

  9. Install the front subframe mudguard assembly. SeeFront Subframe Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation
