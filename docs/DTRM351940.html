NFC Module - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. SeePower-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the outside rearview mirror left holder assembly. SeeOutside Rearview Mirror Left Holder Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove 4 fixing screws, and take out the left rearview mirror protective cover.

  4. Remove 2 fixing screws.

  5. Disconnect one connector, and take out the NFC module.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
