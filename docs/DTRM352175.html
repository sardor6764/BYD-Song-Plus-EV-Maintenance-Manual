Left Front Door Module - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the left front door inner snap handle cover. See Left Front Door Inner Snap Handle Cover - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the left front door inner handle cover. See Left Front Door Inner Handle Cover - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the left front door guard with accessory assembly. See Left Front Door Guard with Accessory Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the left front door inner waist belt. See Left Front Door Inner Waist Belt - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the left front door glass assembly. See Left Front Door Glass Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the left front door bracket assembly. See Left Front Door Bracket Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Pry up 5 fixing clips.

  9. Disconnect one connector.

  10. Take out 2 seals.

  11. Remove 12 fixing bolts.

    • Tightening torque: 9N • m

  12. Release 2 clamping jaws.

  13. Remove one through-hole rubber sleeve, and take out the left front door module.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
