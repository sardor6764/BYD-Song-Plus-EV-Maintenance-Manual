Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • This vehicle is equipped with a supplemental restraint system (SRS), including front double airbags, side airbags and side air curtains. If the operation is not carried out in the correct order, the airbag may be accidentally deployed during the maintenance process, and serious accidents may be caused. Before the maintenance (including removal or installation, inspection or replacement of parts), read the precautions for supplemental restraint system.

  • The electric power steering system of this vehicle has active return control function and remote driving function. After the steering system (gear rack electric column assembly) is replaced, re position the four wheels, calibrate the angle signal, and calibrate the ESP angle signal. Set the power state to “on” and clear the residual DTC.

  • Keep the starter iron battery in good condition, of which low battery will lead to heavy steering.

  • When working on the electric power steering column, disconnect the negative pole of starter iron battery.

  • Before disconnecting the universal joint assembly, keep the wheels in dead ahead, and fix the steering wheel to prevent movement. The clock spring on the electric power steering column may deviate from the center position, and even be damaged due to too many turns of steering wheel.

  • After fitting back the steering system, comprehensively check the portability and flexibility of the steering. When turning the steering wheel, it shall not be too heavy or too loose.

  • Never strike the electric power steering column, especially the sensor, EPS electronic control unit, EPS motor and reduction mechanism. If the electric power steering column falls or suffers serious impact, replace it with a new assembly.

  • Before disconnecting the electric power steering column or intermediate shaft from the steering gear, keep the wheels in dead ahead, make the vehicle power-off. Otherwise, the clock spring on the electric power steering column may deviate from the center position, and even be damaged.

  • Before disconnecting the electric power steering column or intermediate shaft, power off the vehicle. Never move the wheels after disconnecting the above components. Some parts may not be positioned correctly.

  • The duration of turning the steering wheel to the limit position shall not exceed 5s. The power assisted motor may be damaged.

Warning

  • Before the angle signal calibration, never drive the vehicle. Serious damage may be caused.

  • Never touch the steering wheel during calibration with the VDS. The steering wheel shall not be affected by external forces. Serious damage may be caused.

  • When moving the electric power steering column, never pull the wiring harness.
