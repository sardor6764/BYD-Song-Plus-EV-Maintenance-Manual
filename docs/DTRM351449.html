Disassembly of rear shock absorber strut assembly

Reminder
The disassembly methods of the left and right rear shock absorber strut assemblies are the same. Take the left rear shock absorber strut assembly as an example.

Disassembly

  1. Fix the rear shock absorber strut assembly on the workbench.

  2. Compress the rear shock absorber coil spring with a special tool (tool name: xxxx; material number: xxxx) until the rear shock absorber coil spring is separated from the upper seat of the rear shock absorber spring. See the Manual of Special Tools

  3. Remove the rear shock absorber dust cover.

  4. Remove the lock nut with a special tool (tool name: front and rear shock absorber nut remover; material number xxxx). See the Manual of Special Tools

  5. Remove the upper support combination of the rear shock absorber.

  6. Remove the upper seat of the rear shock absorber spring.

  7. Remove the rear shock absorber coil spring.

  8. Remove the rear shock absorber dust cover.

  9. Remove the rear shock absorber buffer body combination.

  10. Remove the lower cushion of rear shock absorber spring.

  11. Take out the rear shock absorber damper assembly.

Assembly

Caution

  • Fit the lower end of the coil spring into the groove of the lower cushion of left rear shock absorber spring .

  • Rotate and align the upper support combination of the rear shock absorber.

  1. Follow the disassembly steps in reverse order to assemble.
