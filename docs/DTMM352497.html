Safe Maintenance

Personnel Requirements

  1. Personnel settings.

    1. Maintenance technicians, i.e. maintenance personnel with BEV and HEV maintenance qualification, are allowed to conduct vehicle inspection and maintenance, as follows:

      • Routine service work.

      • Inspection and maintenance of non-high voltage components.

      • Inspection and maintenance of high voltage circuit.

    2. Supervisors, i.e. maintenance technician with BEV and HEV maintenance qualification, are responsible for supervising the whole process of maintenance, as follows:

      • Supervise whether the composition of maintenance personnel, use of tools, wearing of protective equipment, safety of spare parts, and safety warning signs meet the requirements.

      • Power-on and power-off of high voltage/check the ON and OFF of vehicle power.

      • Check the safe maintenance operating procedures. Command the operation according to the safe maintenance operating procedures. The maintenance personnel shall inform the supervisor after completing an operation.

      • The supervisor shall ensure the safety of the maintenance process and avoid safety accidents.

    3. In addition to the maintenance technician, a supervisor must be assigned for the detection and maintenance of high voltage circuit.

  2. Personnel qualification.

    1. A maintenance technician of new energy vehicle shall meet the following requirements:

      • Get a nationally recognized Special Operation Certificate for Low Voltage Electrical Operation.

      • Attend BYD high voltage training course and obtain corresponding maintenance qualification.

Equipment Requirements

  1. Basic equipment.

    1. "General Equipment" and "Sheet Metal and Painting Equipment" in the maintenance workshop.

  2. Special equipment.

    1. "High Voltage Maintenance Equipment" and "Special Tools" for vehicle maintenance.

  3. Protective equipment.

    1. "Personal Protective Equipment" for maintenance of high voltage components.

  4. Equipment inspection.

    1. Before each use of insulation equipment and safety protection equipment, ensure there is no damage, metal puncture or other damage. If any, never use it.

    2. Check the insulation equipment and safety protection equipment for moisture, water and dirt before each use. If so, use them when their performance is restored.

    3. Check the insulation resistance at the weakest part of the insulation equipment with a megger at 1000V DC every month. If it is less than 2m Ω, stop use it.

High Voltage Identification

  1. High voltage line.

    1. The orange wiring harnesses are high voltage wires.

  2. High voltage components.

    1. All modules connected to the high voltage wiring harness of vehicle.

    2. High voltage components include (but not limited to): power battery pack assembly, electric hybrid system, front drive motor, rear drive motor, drive motor control module, dual electronic control, high voltage distribution box, two-way vehicle power assembly, DC multiplexing AC charging vehicle socket assembly, DC charging distribution box, electric compressor assembly, and integrated PTC air heater assembly.

Maintenance Preparation

  1. Personnel and protective equipment.

    1. Assign personnel and provide protective equipment according to maintenance items.

      • The maintenance technician can work independently. The operation specification is the same as that of the fuel vehicles. The maintenance manual shall be followed.

  2. Overhaul of high voltage circuit.

    1. No power battery leakage:

      • Staffing: maintenance technician + supervisor.

      • Protective equipment: insulating shoes + insulating gloves + insulating pads.

    2. Leakage of power battery:

      • Staffing: maintenance technician + supervisor.

      • When disconnecting the power battery bus, wear the protective equipment: insulating shoes + insulating gloves + insulating pads.

      • When removing the power battery, wear the protective equipment: insulating shoes + acid and alkali resistant gloves + safety goggles + insulating pads.

  3. Maintenance tools.

    1. Service and non-high voltage circuit overhaul.

      • Select basic tools + special tools according to overhaul items.

    2. Overhaul of high voltage circuit.

      • Select basic tools + special tools + insulation tools according to overhaul items.

  4. Maintenance site.

    1. Before the maintenance, take isolation measures (use warning fences) and set high voltage warning signs to warn relevant personnel and avoid safety accidents.

    2. If possible, operate on the new energy maintenance station and connect the vehicle body with the protective ground line.

Overhaul of Dead High Voltage Circuit

  1. Wear protective equipment as required.

  2. Disconnect the negative pole of starter iron battery.

  3. Set the vehicle power to OFF and stand for 5 min.

  4. Disconnect the power maintenance switch (if any).

  5. Disconnect the positive and negative buses of the power battery.

  6. Insulate and seal the power battery positive and negative connectors and wiring harness connectors with insulating tape to prevent short circuit and entry of foreign matters.

  7. Overhaul the high voltage line.

    1. After cutting off the high voltage, wait for at least 5min, and measure the voltage between the positive and negative poles (with one hand) with a multimeter (power battery high voltage plug) before testing the peripheral high voltage component. The voltage shall be less than 60VDC.

    2. When the measured voltage is less than 60V DC, the high voltage lines and components outside the power battery can be normally detected. When the measured voltage is greater than 60V DC, do not operate until the voltage drops below 60V DC.

    3. To disconnect an high voltage connector, insulate and seal it with insulating tape to prevent short circuit and foreign matters.

  8. Restore the high voltage.

Overhaul of Live High Voltage Circuit

  1. Overhaul the charging fault.

    1. Wear protective equipment as required.

    2. Connect the vehicle body to the protective ground line.

    3. See the Maintenance Manual. Measure the parameters according to the fault conditions. Follow the following principles in the measurement process:

      • When using a multimeter to measure high voltage, select the correct range. The accuracy of the multimeter for detection shall not be lower than grade 0.5. It is required to have an AC/DC voltage measurement gear. For the AC input detection of the charging equipment, the AC range shall be greater than 400VAC. For the DC output detection, the range shall be greater than the DC gear of the total voltage of the power battery of the tested vehicle.

      • One probe wire of the multimeter and other test equipment used shall be equipped with an insulated crocodile clip (the voltage withstand is 3kV and the overcurrent capacity is greater than 5A). When measuring, first clamp the crocodile clip to one terminal of the circuit, and then then connect the other probe to the terminal to be measured. Use one hand to hold the probe during each measurement. During the measurement, never operate with both hands or touch the metal part of the probe.

    4. Replacement of high voltage components and wiring harness connectors requires insulation and sealing protection of disconnected connectors to prevent short circuit and foreign matters.

    5. Never disconnect the high/low voltage connectors when the load is in live work. It may cause personal injury and damage to the vehicle.

    6. Never disconnect the low voltage connector when the power is ON. Otherwise the vehicle may be damaged.

    7. For vehicles requiring tooling for high voltage detection, live measurement without tooling is prohibited.

  2. Overhaul the high voltage line.

    1. Wear protective equipment as required.

    2. See the Maintenance Manual. Measure the parameters according to the fault conditions. Follow the following principles in the measurement process:

      • When using a multimeter to measure high voltage, select the correct range. The accuracy of the multimeter for detection shall not be lower than grade 0.5. It is required to have a DC voltage measurement gear. The range selection shall be greater than the DC gear of the total voltage of the power battery of the tested vehicle.

      • One probe wire of the multimeter and other test equipment used shall be equipped with an insulated crocodile clip (the voltage withstand is 3kV and the overcurrent capacity is greater than 5A). When measuring, first clamp the crocodile clip to one terminal of the circuit, and then then connect the other probe to the terminal to be measured. Use one hand to hold the probe during each measurement. During the measurement, never operate with both hands or touch the metal part of the probe.

    3. Replacement of high voltage components and wiring harness connectors requires insulation and sealing protection of disconnected connectors to prevent short circuit and foreign matters.

    4. Never disconnect the high/low voltage connectors when the load is in live work. It may cause personal injury and damage to the vehicle.

    5. Never disconnect the low voltage connector when the power is ON. Otherwise the vehicle may be damaged.

    6. For vehicles requiring tooling for high voltage detection, live measurement without tooling is prohibited.

  3. Overhaul the power battery.

    1. Wear protective equipment as required.

    2. Disconnect the negative pole of starter iron battery.

    3. Set the vehicle power to OFF and stand for 5 min.

    4. Disconnect the power maintenance switch (if any).

    5. Disconnect the positive and negative buses of the power battery.

    6. Insulate and seal the power battery positive and negative connectors and wiring harness connectors with insulating tape to prevent short circuit and entry of foreign matters.

    7. In case of liquid leakage, deformation, water ingress and damage of the power battery, handle it according to the emergency instructions.

    8. The power battery must be lifted up and down with a forklift whose weighting capacity is 1.5 times higher than the total weight of the power battery. The power battery should not be placed askew to ensure safe operation.

    9. Check the power battery for electric leakage.

    10. Detect other faults of power battery. See the Maintenance Manual. Measure the parameters according to the fault conditions. Follow the following principles in the measurement process:

      • When using a multimeter to measure high voltage, select the correct range. The accuracy of the multimeter for detection shall not be lower than grade 0.5. It is required to have a DC voltage measurement gear. The range selection shall be greater than the DC gear of the total voltage of the power battery of the tested vehicle.

      • One probe wire of the multimeter and other test equipment used shall be equipped with an insulated crocodile clip (the voltage withstand is 3kV and the overcurrent capacity is greater than 5A). When measuring, first clamp the crocodile clip to one terminal of the circuit, and then then connect the other probe to the terminal to be measured. Use one hand to hold the probe during each measurement. During the measurement, never operate with both hands or touch the metal part of the probe.

    11. Replacement of high voltage components and wiring harness connectors requires insulation and sealing protection of disconnected connectors to prevent short circuit and foreign matters.

    12. Never disconnect the high/low voltage connectors when the load is in live work. It may cause personal injury and damage to the vehicle.

    13. Never disconnect the low voltage connector when the power is ON. Otherwise the vehicle may be damaged.

    14. When replacing internal components due to leakage of power battery, wear acid and alkali resistant gloves and safety goggles as required. When removing and installing the wiring harness and connector, wear insulating shoes + insulating gloves + insulating pads.

    15. For vehicles requiring tooling for high voltage detection, live measurement without tooling is prohibited.

Welding Machine Used for Sheet Metal Repair

  1. Wear protective equipment as required.

  2. Disconnect the negative pole of starter iron battery.

  3. Disconnect the negative pole of starter iron battery.

  4. Set the vehicle power to OFF and stand for 5 min.

  5. Disconnect the power maintenance switch (if any).

  6. Disconnect the positive and negative buses of the power battery.

  7. Insulate and seal the power battery positive and negative connectors and wiring harness connectors with insulating tape to prevent short circuit and entry of foreign matters.

  8. If the power battery and high voltage components will be stressed during the body sheet metal correction, remove the power battery or high voltage components and store them safely before the correction.

Welding Machine Not Used for Sheet Metal Repair

If the power battery and high voltage components will be stressed during the body sheet metal correction, remove the power battery or high voltage components and store them safely before the correction.

Surface Grinding

Apply the dry grinding process for surface grinding. And water grinding is not allowed.
