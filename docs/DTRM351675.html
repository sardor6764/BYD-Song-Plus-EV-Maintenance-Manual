Left Rear Brake Caliper Sub-Assembly Aftersales Package - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the left rear wheel assembly. SeeWheel Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Discharge the brake fluid. See Discharge and filling brake fluid

  3. Remove the EPB motor drive assembly. SeeEPB Motor Drive Assembly - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove one hollow bolt, and move away the left rear brake hose assembly.

    Caution
    Flat washers are disposable parts that need to be scrapped after removal, and new parts should be used during installation.

    • Tightening torque: 32±3N•m

  5. Remove 2 fixing bolts, take out the left rear brake caliper sub-assembly aftersales package.

    • Tightening torque: 30±3N•m

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.

  2. Add the brake fluid and bleed the brake system. SeeExhaust of Brake System
