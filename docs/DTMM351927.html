Front Drive Electric Assembly

System Composition

The front drive electric assembly is mainly composed of the front drive motor control module, front drive motor, transmission assembly, etc. The front drive motor contains a rotary transformer and a temperature sensor.

System Function Principle

  1. Front drive motor control module

    • Convert the high voltage DC to three-phase AC, receive the vehicle control command, change the AC frequency and direction, so as to control the vehicle movement.

    • When the braking energy is recovered, the three-phase alternating current is converted into high voltage direct current to charge the power battery pack.

  2. Front drive motor

    • It is subject to the front drive motor control module, which drives the vehicle.

    • When the braking energy is recovered, the kinetic energy is converted into electric energy and transmitted to the front drive motor control module.

    • During DC charging, the DC voltage is boosted by the coil of the front drive motor to provide high voltage DC power for the power batteries.

    • Measure the drive motor temperature.

  3. Rotary transformer

    • The rotary transformer is used to measure the rotation angle and position.

    • The position and speed of motor rotor are detected in real time to realize the closed-loop control of torque and speed.

    • The shield grounding cable of the rotary transformer shall be well connected with the vehicle grounding to prevent the signal error. The rotary transformer shield grounding is a kind of grounding cable for drive motor.
