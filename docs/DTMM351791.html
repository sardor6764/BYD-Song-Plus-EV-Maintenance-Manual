Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • Never remove any part of the system or its connector at will to avoid damage.

  • The reliability of the parts of the vehicle control system is high. When the vehicle or the drive motor is abnormal, first check whether the relevant mechanical parts, system connectors, wiring harnesses and the ground lines are in good contact.

  • The faults of the vehicle control system are mainly wiring harness and connector problems, generally including broken wiring harness, poor connector contact, unplugged connector terminal, grounding of parts, etc.

  • It is rare for the wiring harness to break in the middle, mostly at the connector. Therefore, the wiring harness at the sensor and connector shall be checked carefully.

  • Poor contact may be caused by the corrosion of the connector terminal, the entry of external dirt into the terminal, or the reduction of contact pressure between the plug and socket. Replugging the connector can change its connection state and restore normal contact.

  • During fault diagnosis, if the wiring harness and connector are normal and the fault disappears after reconnection, the wiring or connector may be considered as faulty.

  • Never check the electrical signal of the system by piercing the wiring harness with a fine needle.

  • Tighten the fixing bolts or nuts to the specified torque.

  • During the arc welding of the vehicle, disconnect the positive and negative cables of the starter iron battery. If necessary, remove the vehicle control unit and other control units to avoid damage caused by high voltage. When carrying out body repair near the vehicle control unit or sensor, avoid damaging these electronic components.

  • When installing or removing the vehicle control unit, the operator shall first ground himself to remove the static electricity. Otherwise, it may damage the vehicle control unit circuit.

  • The power system is grounded at the negative pole. When installing the starter iron battery, never connect the positive pole and negative pole reversely to avoid damaging the electronic components.

  • If a fault occurs in the system, and the fault is not eliminated after the VDS clears the DTC, the DTC will remain until the fault is eliminated. The historical DTC shall be cleared after the maintenance is completed.

Warning

  • When the parts of the vehicle control system are detected to be damaged, replace them as a whole. Never disassemble or remove them. Other parts may be affected.

  • Before overhauling the vehicle control unit, set the power state to OFF, and turn off all electrical loads. Disconnect the negative pole of the starter iron battery.

  • When replacing the vehicle control unit, handle it with care and ensure it is not damaged. Any impact and drop may greatly affect its performance.
