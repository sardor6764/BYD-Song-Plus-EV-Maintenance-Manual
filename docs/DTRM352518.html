Ceiling Wiring Harness - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System

  2. Remove the curtain assembly. See Cargo Cover Assembly - Removal and Installation

  3. Remove the tailgate frame weather strip. See Tailgate Frame Weather Strip - Removal and Installation

  4. Remove the front interior light assembly. See Front Interior Dome Light Assembly - Removal and Installation

  5. Remove the roof handle assembly. See Roof Handle Assembly - Removal and Installation

  6. Remove the ceiling assembly. See Ceiling Assembly - Removal and Installation

  7. Remove the instrument panel right lower sound absorbing baffle assembly. See Instrument Panel Right Lower Sound Absorbing Baffle Assembly - Removal and Installation

  8. Disconnect all connectors of ceiling wiring harness.

  9. Remove all fixing bolts at the ground point of ceiling wiring harness.

    • Tightening torque: 9N • m

  10. Pry up all fixing clips of ceiling wiring harness, and take out ceiling wiring harness.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
