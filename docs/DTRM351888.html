Front Wash Line Sub-assembly I - Removal and Installation

Removal

  1. Remove the right front wheel mudguard assembly. See Right Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly - Removal and Installation

  2. Remove the front wash line sub-assembly I and the fixing parts of the front compartment cover.

    1. Disconnect the front wash line sub-assembly I from the front wash line sub-assembly II.

      Caution

      Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

    2. Detach 2 fixing clips.

  3. Detach 1 fixing clips.

  4. Detach 2 fixing clips.

  5. Remove the front wash line sub-assembly I.

    1. Detach 1 fixing clips.

    2. Disconnect the front wash line sub-assembly I from the washer reservoir and motor assembly, and take out the front wash line sub-assembly I.

      Caution

      Close the pipeline openings in time to prevent foreign matters from entering.

Installation

  1. Follow the removal steps in reverse order to install.
