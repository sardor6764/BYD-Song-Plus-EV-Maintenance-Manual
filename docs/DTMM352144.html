Precautions

Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution
Important notes on the seat belt system.

  • Never bump the retractor when removing and installing the seat belt assembly.

  • If the seat belt pretensioner (retractor) falls from a height of more than 914mm, replace it.

  • Check that the retractor functions properly after the installation.

  • If the seat belt is damaged, disconnected or the buckle is out of order, replace it immediately.

  • If there is a crash and the seat belt bears a certain impact force, replace the seat belt immediately even if no damage is found.

  • Never insert foreign matters into the buckle part of the seat belt, nor modify or remove the seat belt.

  • When the seat belt is dirty, clean it with neutral detergent. Never use corrosive agent, dye or dry cleaning agent. The strength of the seat belt may be seriously affected.

  • Keep the seat belt strap dry and clean.

  • If the seat belt is damaged, cracked or deformed due to chemical action or sunlight, replace it.

  • If the metal connecting parts of the seat belt are bent, deformed or rusted, replace the seat belt.

  • Performance requirements of retractor: it shall be able to retract freely at the installation position, and can retract and return automatically.

  • Performance requirements of buckle: there must be a clear feeling of being in place when the buckle is inserted and released. The buckle shall not be released by itself and the assembly shall not fail.

  • Never maintain the retractor. Replace the failed assembly with a new one.

Important notes on pre tensioned seat belt systems.

  • Important notes on pre tensioned seat belt systems.

  • Before the maintenance, disconnect the negative pole of the starter iron battery and wait for 90s.

  • During maintenance, if the parts of the supplemental restraint system may be impacted, remove these parts first.

  • When using electric welding, remove the connectors of airbag (gas generator) and seat belt pretensioner (driver and front passenger) respectively.

  • When painting around the supplemental restraint system and its related components, never expose the wires or connectors to the paint mist.

  • Never expose the parts of the supplemental restraint system to the high temperature environment (drying or baking after painting) or near the flame.

  • If the seat belt pretension function is activated, replace the seat belt.

  • For vehicles equipped with seat belt pretensioner, the authorized dealers or service outlets are responsible for the overhaul of the components or lines of the supplemental restraint system and its peripheral components or lines.
