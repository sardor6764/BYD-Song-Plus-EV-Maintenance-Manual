PDC Calibration

  1. Preconditions for PDC calibration: when vehicle off tracking is identified after four-wheel alignment, and the “PDC initial torque” read by the tester from the “data flow” is not “0”, PDC calibration to the vehicle steering system is required.

  2. Matters need attention during calibration:

    1. Before EPS system PDC calibration, four-wheel alignment has been completed, but off-tracking phenomenon still exists.

    2. Never operate the steering wheel or apply any torque on the steering wheel.

    3. After all above conditions are met, the calibration personnel shall tap the PDC calibration command in the EPS calibration equipment to start the PDC calibration of the EPS system.

    4. After the calibration, repower on the vehicle, check whether the “PDC initial torque value” in “data flow” of EPS is “0”, if YES, the calibration is succeeded, if NO, the calibration is failed, and recalibration is required.

    5. If the calibration is succeeded, check the EPS system for DTC and for normal working.
