Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • Wear insulating gloves, insulating shoes, goggles and other insulating protective equipment.

  • Before maintaining high voltage components, follow the standard procedure to disable high voltage power.

  • After disconnecting the high voltage component connector, use a multimeter to measure its voltage. The next step can be carried out only after the voltage drops to the safe voltage.

  • When maintaining high voltage components, note the surface temperature of the housing and operate after it cools down.

  • After removing the high voltage wiring harness, provide insulation protection at the high voltage wiring harness and high voltage components interfaces.

Warning

  • Nonprofessionals are not allowed to maintain and replace high voltage system components.

  • Maintenance technicians without high voltage safety training are not allowed to maintain and operate high voltage components.

  • Never maintain or replace high voltage components during vehicle charging.

  • Before working on high voltage components, disconnect the charging port and the charging equipment, and ensure that the vehicle is powered off.

  • When measuring the insulation resistance, never touch the exposed conductive parts. There may be a risk of electric shock.

  • Never maintain the vehicle in corrosive, flammable and explosive gas environment. It may cause fire and explosion.

  • Without permission, never remove or modify the high voltage components. The high voltage components may fail and dangerous accidents may be caused.
