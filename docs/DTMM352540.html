Operation Safety Precautions and Warnings

Caution

  • When disconnecting the cooling system pipeline, use a special container to collect residual coolant.

  • The coolant is toxic. If it is not used, keep it away from children and pets and dispose of it as required.

  • When disconnecting the cooling system pipeline connector, to prevent dirt or impurities from entering, seal the pipe orifice with a plug.

  • When changing the coolant, lay a cloth in the front compartment to absorb the spilled coolant.

  • Different brands of coolants have different formulas. If they are mixed, chemical reactions between additives are likely to occur, resulting in coolant failure.

  • When draining and filling the coolant, take protective measures to prevent the coolant from polluting the parts.

  • When replacing the coolant, drain the old coolant, clean the cooling system, and then add new coolant.

  • Use acceptable coolant.

  • Dispose of used coolant according to local laws and regulations.

  • When the temperature is lower than 0°C, regularly check the freezing point of the coolant.

Warning

  • Never open the expansion tank cover when the engine is hot. High-temperature coolant or steam may spray out, causing personal injury. The expansion tank cover can only be opened when the coolant temperature drops below 40 °C.

  • When disconnecting the cooling system pipelines, prevent debris to enter the cooling system pipelines. Otherwise, the cooling system may be damaged.
