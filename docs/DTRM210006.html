Wheel rotation

Caution

  • To ensure even tire wear and to prolong the service life of tires, it is recommended that wheel rotation shall be carried out in accordance with the maintenance schedule. However, the proper time for wheel rotation depends on driver's driving habits and road conditions.

  • After wheel rotation, the tire pressure monitoring module matching and learning shall be conducted.

Because the load borne by the front and rear wheels of the vehicle is different during driving, the wear situation will be different. Therefore, in order to avoid tire wear in a single direction, regular and timely transposition can make the tire wear even, thus extending the tire life. It is recommended to change positions every 5000-8000km.The main purpose of tire transposition is :

  • Ensure uniform tire wear and average fatigue, thus ensuring stability and economy.

  • Check the condition of tires during transposition .It can find out the damage in time and prevent accidents.

  • When replacing tires, it is necessary to ensure that the steering tires are in the best condition.It can improve the vehicle's stability and safety.

Wheel Rotation in the Cross Pattern

  1. Remove 4 wheel assemblies.

  2. Install the 1 # wheel to the original mounting position of the 4 # wheel.

  3. Install the 4 # wheel to the original mounting position of the 2 # wheel.

  4. Install the 2 # wheel to the original mounting position of the 3 # wheel.

  5. Install the 3 # wheel to the original mounting position of the 1 # wheel.
